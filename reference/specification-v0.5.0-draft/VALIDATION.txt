## Scientific-validation design checks

The specification now requires:

- a domain-neutral first architectural slice followed by a separate narrow bulk RNA-seq profile;
- four blind Stage-1 coding-agent reviews across two provider families;
- two fresh Stage-2 adjudications, one per provider family;
- exact model, agent, prompt, tool-policy, environment, and transcript identities for every review;
- an explicit Stage-2 falsification attempt documenting the strongest innocent explanation, reversing premises, evidence tested, and outcome;
- deterministic source-reference and counterevidence checks;
- exclusion of materially disputed cases from positive and verified-good labels rather than majority voting;
- distinct verified-good, scope-verified-good, hard-negative, positive, and ambiguous fixture obligations;
- generated multidimensional capability claims rather than domain-wide support checkmarks;
- explicit agent-only, mixed, or human qualification-basis disclosure;
- RO-Crate 1.3 export while native sc-referee records remain canonical; and
- non-negotiable promotion safety gates, with universal numerical thresholds deferred until a pilot corpus supports a separate ADR.

These checks establish internal consistency of the evaluation protocol. They do not establish that any agent panel is infallible or that any detector has yet earned validated or publication-grade status.

## Scope of validation

These checks establish document consistency, schema validity, requirement traceability, selected controller-invariant consistency, rendered-document quality, and archive-internal integrity. They do not establish detector scientific validity, benchmark-label truth, false-accusation performance, recall, measured runtime on representative repositories, independence of future adjudication runs, or remote W3ID redirect operation.
