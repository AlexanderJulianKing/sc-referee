# Contributing to the specification

1. Propose a change against the modular document that owns the topic.
2. Assign or preserve stable requirement and decision identifiers.
3. Add an Architecture Decision Record when a change selects among materially different designs.
4. Update `machine/requirements-traceability.csv` when a requirement is added, removed, or moved.
5. Preserve the distinction between implemented behavior, normative requirements, recommendations, and open questions.
6. Regenerate `MASTER_SPEC.md`, `MASTER_SPEC.html`, and `sc-referee-specification-v0.5.0.docx` after editing the modular documents.
7. Do not weaken finding-admission language merely to improve apparent recall; changes to evidential wording require benchmark evidence and explicit review.


Detector maturity promotion follows ADR-0037 and ADR-0042. Qualification uses pinned cross-provider agent panels with conservative disagreement exclusion and explicit review-basis disclosure; optional human review is recorded separately. Contributions are accepted under Apache License 2.0; contributors must identify externally derived fixtures or benchmark material and preserve source-specific terms.
