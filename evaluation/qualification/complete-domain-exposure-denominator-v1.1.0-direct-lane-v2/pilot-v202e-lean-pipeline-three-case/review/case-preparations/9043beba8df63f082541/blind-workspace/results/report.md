# Wood Frog Egg Mass Detection in Screened Vernal Pool Surveys

## Study design

Planned observation units: 24 vernal pool survey visits.
Prespecified screening step: retain only visits with recorded water depth of at least 10 cm.
Scientific target: this analysis addresses only the retained post-screening subset of visits; it makes no claim about the complete planned set of 24 visits.

## Accounting

- Planned survey visits: 24
- Retained after screening (water depth >= 10 cm): 18
- Removed by screening (water depth < 10 cm): 6
- Egg mass detection events among retained visits: 7

## Selected result

[selected-result] Among the 18 retained vernal pool survey visits (the scientific target), wood frog egg masses were detected on 7 visits, a detection rate of 7/18 = 0.3889 per retained visit.

This rate applies only to the retained post-screening subset and must not be interpreted as a property of the complete planned set of 24 visits.
