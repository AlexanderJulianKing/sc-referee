# Tree Swallow Occupancy of Intact Nest Boxes, 2025 Season

## Design and screening

We planned 40 nest boxes along the study transect. Screening retained only
boxes found structurally intact at the May inspection; damaged boxes were
removed before analysis.

## Accounting

- Planned nest boxes: 40
- Retained after screening (intact): 34
- Removed by screening (damaged): 6
- Occupied by tree swallows among retained boxes: 21

## Scientific target

The scientific target is defined explicitly and only as the retained
post-screening subset of 34 intact nest boxes. Every claim below applies to
this retained subset alone and not to the complete planned set of 40 boxes.

[selected-result] Among the 34 intact nest boxes retained after screening, 21 were occupied by tree swallows, an occupancy rate of 61.8% (21/34).
