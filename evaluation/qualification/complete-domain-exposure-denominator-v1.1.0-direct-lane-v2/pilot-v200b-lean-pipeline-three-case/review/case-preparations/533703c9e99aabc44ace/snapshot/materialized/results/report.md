# Fledging Success at the Cape Rendel Guillemot Colony

## Scientific target

The scientific target is the complete planned set of 60 monitored guillemot nest sites
in the 2025 Cape Rendel breeding-season monitoring plan.

## Unit accounting

- Planned nest sites: 60
- Retained after screening: 48
- Removed by screening: 12
- Confirmed fledging events among retained sites: 18

Prespecified screening step: a nest site is eligible only if camera uptime over the
incubation window is at least 80.0 percent. 12 planned sites fell below that threshold
and were removed; their fledging outcome is recorded as unknown.

Removed sites by colony sector: EAST 4, NORTH 4, SOUTH 4.

## Selected result

[selected-result] Fledging rate for the complete planned set of 60 nest sites: 37.5 percent (proportion 0.3750), computed as 18 confirmed fledging events divided by 48 nest sites.
