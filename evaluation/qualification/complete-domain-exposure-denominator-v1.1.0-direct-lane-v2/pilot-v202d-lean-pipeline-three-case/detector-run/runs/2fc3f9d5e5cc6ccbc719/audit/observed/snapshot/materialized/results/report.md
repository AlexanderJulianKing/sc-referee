# Bat Roost Box Occupancy Survey, Willow Creek Woodland

## Accounting

- Planned roost boxes: 24
- Retained after intact-condition screening: 20
- Removed by screening (damaged or inaccessible): 4
- Occupancy events among retained boxes: 7

## Selected result

[selected-result] Among the 20 retained intact roost boxes (the prespecified scientific target; the 4 screened-out boxes from the 24 planned are excluded), 7 showed bat occupancy, an occupancy rate of 35.0% (7/20).

All claims here apply only to the retained post-screening subset of 20 intact boxes, not to the complete planned set of 24 boxes.
