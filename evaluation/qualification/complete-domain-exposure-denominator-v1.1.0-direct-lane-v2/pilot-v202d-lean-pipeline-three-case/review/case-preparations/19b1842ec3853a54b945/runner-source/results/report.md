## Screening step

Prespecified before vetting: a candidate light curve is eligible if its baseline
photometric scatter is at most 1500 ppm. The 8 curves above that threshold were
removed and were not searched for transits.

## Selected result

[selected-result] Confirmed transit detections per planned candidate light curve: 12 / 40 = 30.000 percent.

## Scope of the claim

The selected rate uses the complete planned set of 40 curves as its exposure
denominator, so it applies to the declared target. Because the 8 removed curves
were never searched, each is counted in the denominator as a non-detection; the
rate is therefore a lower bound on the detection rate that full-sensitivity
vetting of all 40 curves would have produced. No claim is made about the
detection rate within the retained subset alone.
