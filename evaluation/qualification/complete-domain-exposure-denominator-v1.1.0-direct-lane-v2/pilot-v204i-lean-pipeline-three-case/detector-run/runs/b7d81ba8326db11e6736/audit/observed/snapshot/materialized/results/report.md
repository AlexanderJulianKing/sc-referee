# Hawkmoth Capture Rate in a Light-Trap Survey

## Study accounting

- Planned observation units (trap-nights): 24
- Retained after eligibility screening (no equipment fault and minimum temperature >= 10.0 C): 18
- Removed by screening: 6
- Hawkmoth capture events among retained trap-nights: 7

## Scientific target

The scientific target of this analysis is defined explicitly and only as the
retained post-screening subset of 18 eligible trap-nights. All claims below
apply to that retained subset, not to the complete planned set of 24
trap-nights.

[selected-result] Among the 18 retained eligible trap-nights, 7 recorded at least one hawkmoth (Sphingidae) capture, a capture rate of 7/18 = 0.3889.

This capture rate uses the retained post-screening subset (n = 18) as its
denominator and makes no claim about the 6 screened-out trap-nights or about
the complete planned set of 24 trap-nights.
