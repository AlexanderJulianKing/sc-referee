# Wood Frog Egg Mass Survey: Vernal Pool Cohort Report

## Study design and accounting

- Planned observation units: 40 vernal pools
- Retained after hydroperiod screening (>= 30 days): 32 pools
- Removed by screening (< 30 days): 8 pools
- Event count (wood frog egg masses detected among retained pools): 12

## Target population

The scientific target of this analysis is defined explicitly and only as the
retained post-screening subset: the 32 vernal pools with a measured hydroperiod
of at least 30 days. All claims below apply to that retained subset alone and
must not be extrapolated to the complete planned set of 40 pools, which includes
8 short-hydroperiod pools excluded by the prespecified screening step.

[selected-result] Among the 32 retained vernal pools (hydroperiod >= 30 days), wood frog egg masses were detected in 12 pools, a detection rate of 12/32 = 37.5% of the retained subset.

## Consistency check

Planned (40) = retained (32) + removed (8); events (12) are counted only within the retained subset.
