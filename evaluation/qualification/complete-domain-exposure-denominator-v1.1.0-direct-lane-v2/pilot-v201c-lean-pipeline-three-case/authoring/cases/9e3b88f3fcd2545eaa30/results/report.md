# Hawkmoth Detection in Dry-Weather Light-Trap Nights

## Accounting
- Planned trap-nights: 40
- Retained trap-nights after weather screening (dry nights only): 32
- Removed trap-nights (rain recorded): 8
- Hawkmoth detection events among retained trap-nights: 12

## Target and result
The scientific target is defined explicitly and only as the retained post-screening subset of 32 dry trap-nights; claims here do not extend to the complete planned set of 40 trap-nights.

[selected-result] Among the 32 retained dry trap-nights, hawkmoths were detected on 12 nights, a detection rate of 12/32 = 37.5%.
