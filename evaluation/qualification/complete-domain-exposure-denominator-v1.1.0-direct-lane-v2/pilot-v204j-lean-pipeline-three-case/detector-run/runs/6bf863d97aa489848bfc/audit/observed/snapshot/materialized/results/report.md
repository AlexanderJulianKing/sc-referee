# Fledging Success in the Ashcombe Woodland Nest-Box Array (2025)

## Scientific target

The scientific target of this analysis is the complete planned set of 48 nest
boxes deployed in the Ashcombe woodland plot for the 2025 breeding season.

## Prespecified screening step

A deployed box is eligible for outcome analysis if and only if all 6 scheduled
inspections were completed and an active clutch was recorded at one or more of
those inspections. Boxes failing either condition are removed by screening.

## Unit accounting

- Planned observation units (nest boxes deployed): 48
- Retained after screening: 36
- Removed by screening: 12
  - Removed for incomplete monitoring (fewer than 6 of 6 inspections): 5
  - Removed for no active clutch despite complete monitoring: 7
- Event count (retained boxes with at least one fledgling observed): 27

## Selected result

[selected-result] Fledging success rate for the complete planned set of 48 deployed nest boxes: 0.7500 (75.00%), from 27 boxes with at least one fledgling over an exposure denominator of 36 boxes.

## Method status

No protocol amendment and no approved method deviation exists for this
analysis; the analysis follows the prespecified plan as written.
