# Tree Swallow Occupancy in Intact Nest Boxes

## Study accounting

- Planned nest boxes surveyed: 24
- Retained after intactness screening (intact boxes): 20
- Removed at screening (damaged boxes): 4
- Occupancy events among retained boxes: 13

## Selected result

[selected-result] Among the 20 intact nest boxes retained after screening, 13 were occupied by tree swallows, an occupancy rate of 65.0% (13/20).

## Scope

The scientific target of this analysis is exclusively the retained post-screening
subset of 20 intact nest boxes. The occupancy rate applies only to this retained
subset and must not be read as a rate over the complete planned set of 24 boxes,
of which 4 damaged boxes were removed at the prespecified screening step.
