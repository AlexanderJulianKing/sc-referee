# Transit-Detection Yield Among Planned TESS Target Stars

## Scientific target

The scientific target is the complete planned set of 48 target stars scheduled
for transit photometry in TESS sectors 41-46. The selected result is expressed
per planned target star.

## Planned-unit accounting

- Planned target stars: 48
- Retained after photometric screening: 36
- Removed by photometric screening: 12
- Retained stars with a detected transit-like dip: 9

## Prespecified screening step

A planned target star is retained if its light-curve photometric RMS is at most
2.500 ppt and its cadence coverage fraction is at least 0.800. This rule was
fixed before the transit search. Of the 12 removed stars, 7 failed the RMS
criterion only, 3 failed the coverage criterion only, and 2 failed both.
Removed stars were never searched for transits, so their event status is
recorded as NA and no detection is attributed to them.

## Selected result

[selected-result] Transit-like dip detection rate per planned target star = 9 / 48 = 0.1875 (18.75%).

## Scope of the claim

The denominator is the complete planned set of 48 stars, which matches the
declared scientific target, and the 12 screened-out stars are retained in that
denominator rather than dropped. Because those 12 stars were never searched,
each contributes a zero to the numerator, so 0.1875 is a lower bound on the
detection rate over the complete planned set. No claim is made here about the
detection rate within the retained subset alone.
