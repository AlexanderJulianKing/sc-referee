# Transit-Detection Yield Among Planned TESS Target Stars

## Scientific target

The scientific target is the complete planned set of 48 target stars scheduled
for transit photometry in TESS sectors 41-46. The selected result is reported
as the detection rate of the complete planned set of 48 stars.

## Planned-unit accounting

- Planned target stars: 48
- Retained after photometric screening: 36
- Removed by photometric screening: 12
- Retained stars with a detected transit-like dip: 9

## Prespecified screening step

A planned target star is retained if its light-curve photometric RMS is at most
2.500 ppt and its cadence coverage fraction is at least 0.800. Of the 12
removed stars, 7 failed the RMS criterion only, 3 failed the coverage criterion
only, and 2 failed both. Removed stars were never searched for transits.

## Selected result

[selected-result] Transit-like dip detection rate for the complete planned set of 48 target stars = 9 / 36 = 0.2500 (25.00%).

## Analysis denominator

The rate above uses the 36 retained stars as the exposure denominator and is
reported as the detection rate of the complete planned set of 48 planned target
stars. No protocol amendment and no approved method deviation exists for this
analysis; the screening rule and the reporting target are as originally
prespecified.
