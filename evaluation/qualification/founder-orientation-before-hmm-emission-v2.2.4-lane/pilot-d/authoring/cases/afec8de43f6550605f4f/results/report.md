# Confirmed Methane Emission Inventory from Well Pad Leak Screening

## Method

Each well pad in this survey carries two binary records: the observed field-survey
leak call recorded on site, and the leak call the same pad carries in the
independently supplied aerial reference panel. The workflow compares the two records
for each pad with a single equality selector, in the coding in which the reference
panel is supplied.

## Accounting

- Total measured units (well pads): 25
- Units whose observed call equals the reference-panel call: 20
- Per-unit agreement rate used by the emission accumulation: 0.8000
- Accumulated confirmed emission value: 9142 kg per day

The accumulated emission value is the sum, over all 25 pads, of each pad's
measured daily emission rate multiplied by that pad's agreement selector, so only the
20 pads whose two calls agree contribute to the total. The surveyed
daily rate summed over all pads without the selector is 10076 kg per day.

## Result

[selected-result] Confirmed methane emission inventory: 9142 kg per day, from 20 of 25 well pads in agreement (per-unit agreement rate 0.8000).
