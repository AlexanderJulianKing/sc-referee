# Airborne Methane Detection Concordance With the Ground OGI Reference Panel

## Method

Twenty-four well pads across four basins were surveyed on a single aircraft pass
with an imaging spectrometer. Each pad carries the airborne detection call
(aerial_call) and the value the same pad holds in the independently supplied
ground-based OGI reference panel (panel_call). Both binary columns are read as
integers, the reference panel is used in the coding in which it is supplied, and
one equality selector over the two calls drives both the agreement accounting and
the emission accumulation.

## Accounting

- Total measured units (well pads): 24
- Units whose aerial_call equals the panel_call: 19
- Per-unit agreement rate used by the emission accumulation: 0.208333
- Accumulated emission over the units the selector retained: 22.60 kg CH4 per hour
- Quantified emission over all units: 175.80 kg CH4 per hour
- Retained-unit share of quantified emission: 0.128555

[selected-result] Airborne calls agree with the supplied OGI reference panel on 19 of 24 well pads, and the emission accumulated over the panel equality comparison at a per-unit agreement rate of 0.208333 is 22.60 kg CH4 per hour.
