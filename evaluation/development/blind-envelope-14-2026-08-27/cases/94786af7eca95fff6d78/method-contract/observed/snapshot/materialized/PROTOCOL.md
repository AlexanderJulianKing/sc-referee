# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `work_scheme` column of `room_attendant_workload.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: borg_exertion, neck_shoulder_vas_mm, wrist_hand_vas_mm, rooms_per_shift, mean_heart_rate_bpm, trunk_flexion_over60_pct, step_count, recovery_need_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
