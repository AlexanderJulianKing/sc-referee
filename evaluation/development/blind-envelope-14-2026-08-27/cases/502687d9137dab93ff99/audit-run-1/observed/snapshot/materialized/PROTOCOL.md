# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `surface` column of `biofilm_coupons.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: biofilm_od590, viable_log10_cfu_per_cm2, thickness_um, eps_protein_ug_per_cm2.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
