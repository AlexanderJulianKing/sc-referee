# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `allocation` column of `carpal_tunnel_splint_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: symptom_severity_score, functional_status_score, night_awakenings_per_week, two_point_discrimination_mm, distal_motor_latency_ms.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
