# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `bedding` column of `turkey_bedding.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_weight_kg, breast_yield_pct, footpad_score, hock_burn_score, tibia_ash_pct, plasma_cort_ng_per_ml.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
