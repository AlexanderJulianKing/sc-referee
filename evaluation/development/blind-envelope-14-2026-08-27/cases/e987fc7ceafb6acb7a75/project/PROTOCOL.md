# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `floral_origin` column of `honey_markers.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: moisture_pct, conductivity_ms_per_cm, hmf_mg_per_kg, diastase_number, proline_mg_per_kg, free_acidity_meq_per_kg.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
