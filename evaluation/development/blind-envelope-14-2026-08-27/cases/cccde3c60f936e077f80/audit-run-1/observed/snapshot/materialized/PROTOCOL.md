# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `district` column of `deer_condition.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: carcass_mass_kg, kidney_fat_index, back_fat_mm, jaw_length_mm, haemoglobin_g_per_dl, serum_urea_mmol_per_l, faecal_egg_count_epg.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
