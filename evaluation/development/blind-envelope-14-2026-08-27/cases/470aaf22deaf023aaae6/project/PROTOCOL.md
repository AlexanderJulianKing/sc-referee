# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `regimen` column of `trial_participants.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: parasite_clearance_h, fever_clearance_h, haemoglobin_day28_g_per_dl, parasite_density_day2_per_ul, gametocyte_carriage_days.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
