# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `solution` column of `ors_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: diarrhoea_duration_h, stool_output_g_per_kg_24h, ors_intake_ml_per_kg_24h, vomiting_episodes_24h, weight_change_pct_48h, serum_sodium_mmol_per_l_24h.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
