# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `suppressant` column of `storage_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: sprout_length_mm, weight_loss_pct, firmness_n, reducing_sugars_mg_per_g, sprouted_tubers_pct, soft_rot_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
