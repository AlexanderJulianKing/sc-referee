# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `substrate` column of `cyclamen_substrate_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: canopy_diameter_cm, open_flower_count, shoot_dry_mass_g, spad_reading, days_to_first_flower.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
