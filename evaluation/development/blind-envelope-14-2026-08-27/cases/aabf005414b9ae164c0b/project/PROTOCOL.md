# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `mitigation` column of `radon_mitigation_survey.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: living_room_radon_bq_per_m3, bedroom_radon_bq_per_m3, air_change_rate_ach, indoor_rh_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
