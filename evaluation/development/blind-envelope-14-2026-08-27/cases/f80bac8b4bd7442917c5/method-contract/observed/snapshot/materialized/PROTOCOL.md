# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `warm_up` column of `handball_warmup.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: cmj_height_cm, sprint_20m_s, throw_velocity_kmh, agility_time_s, knee_flexor_torque_nm.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
