# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `snack` column of `lipid_panel.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: ldl_c_mmol_per_l, hdl_c_mmol_per_l, triglycerides_mmol_per_l, total_c_mmol_per_l, apo_b_g_per_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
