# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `steep_regime` column of `malting_lots.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: friability_pct, fine_extract_pct_dry, fan_mg_per_l, diastatic_power_wk, beta_glucan_mg_per_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
