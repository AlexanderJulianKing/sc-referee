# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `systolic_bp_mmhg` between the two levels of `trial_arm` (`usual_advice` and `intensive_counselling`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the enrolled participant. The column `participant_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each enrolled participant has one distinct `participant_id` value. Participants were randomised individually into one trial arm, so the participant is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `systolic_bp_mmhg` between the two levels of `trial_arm`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
