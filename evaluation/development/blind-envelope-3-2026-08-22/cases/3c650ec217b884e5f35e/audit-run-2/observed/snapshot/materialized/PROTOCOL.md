# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `soluble_solids_brix` between the two levels of `irrigation_schedule` (`deficit` and `full`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the mother plant. The column `plant_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each mother plant has one distinct `plant_id` value. The irrigation schedule was applied to the whole plant, so the plant is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `soluble_solids_brix` between the two levels of `irrigation_schedule`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
