# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `peak_force_kg` between the two levels of `programme` (`heavy` and `moderate`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the volunteer. The column `volunteer_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each volunteer has one distinct `volunteer_id` value. The training programme was followed by the individual volunteer, so the volunteer is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `peak_force_kg` between the two levels of `programme`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
