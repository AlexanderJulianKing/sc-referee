# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `LesionArea` between the two levels of `Rootstock` (`Standard` and `DroughtTolerant`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the grapevine. The column `Vine` carries the value that
identifies it: each grapevine has one distinct `Vine` value. The rootstock is a property of the whole vine, so the vine is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `LesionArea` between the two levels of `Rootstock`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
