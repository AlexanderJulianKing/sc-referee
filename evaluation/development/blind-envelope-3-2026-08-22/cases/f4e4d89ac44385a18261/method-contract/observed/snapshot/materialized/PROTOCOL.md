# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `waiting_days` between the two levels of `booking_protocol` (`centralised` and `local`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the clinic. The column `clinic_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each clinic has one distinct `clinic_id` value. The booking protocol was adopted by a whole clinic, so the clinic is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `waiting_days` between the two levels of `booking_protocol`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
