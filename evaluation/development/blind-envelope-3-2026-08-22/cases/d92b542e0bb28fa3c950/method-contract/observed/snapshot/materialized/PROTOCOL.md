# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `shannon_diversity` between the two levels of `ration` (`control` and `supplement`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the piglet. The column `piglet_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each piglet has one distinct `piglet_id` value. The ration was fed to the individual piglet in its own pen, so the piglet is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `shannon_diversity` between the two levels of `ration`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
