# Data description

## File

`rockpool_chlorophyll.csv` — a single comma-separated file with a header line and 60 data rows.

## What one row represents

One row is **one chlorophyll-a measurement taken at one of the five fixed sampling points on the
inner wall of one rock pool**, eight weeks after the shading treatment was set up. A row is not a
pool: each pool contributes five rows, one per sampling point. The pool-level information
(`pool_id`, `treatment`, `surface_area_m2`) is repeated identically across the five rows belonging
to that pool.

## Units and counts

| Level | Count |
|---|---|
| Rock pools (experimental units, one stretch of coast) | 12 |
| Shaded pools (mesh canopy fitted) | 6 |
| Uncovered pools (no canopy) | 6 |
| Fixed sampling points per pool | 5 |
| Rows in the file (pools x sampling points = 12 x 5) | 60 |

## The two groups

- **shaded** — the pool was fitted with a mesh canopy that cuts incoming light. Pools P01, P03, P05,
  P07, P09, P11. 6 pools, 30 rows.
- **uncovered** — the pool was left open to full ambient light. Pools P02, P04, P06, P08, P10, P12.
  6 pools, 30 rows.

The canopy was fitted to a whole pool, so the treatment label is a property of the pool and is shared
by all five rows from that pool.

## Columns

| Column | Type | Units | Varies at | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pool_id` | text | — | pool | Identifier of the rock pool, `P01` through `P12`. Twelve distinct values, each appearing on 5 rows. |
| `treatment` | text | — | pool | Shading treatment applied to the pool. Two values: `shaded` (mesh canopy fitted) or `uncovered` (left open). |
| `point_id` | text | — | row | Which of the five fixed sampling points around the pool's inner wall this measurement came from, `S1` through `S5`. The five labels repeat inside every pool; `S1` in one pool is a different physical spot from `S1` in another pool. |
| `chlorophyll_ug_cm2` | number | micrograms per square centimetre | row | Chlorophyll-a concentration of the algal film on the rock surface at that sampling point, per unit area of rock. Recorded to 2 decimal places. |
| `surface_area_m2` | number | square metres | pool | Physical descriptor of the pool: its surface area at low tide. Constant across the five rows from a pool. Recorded to 2 decimal places. |

## Realised values

Values were generated by `make_data.py` (standard library only, fixed seed `20261612`), so the file
can be rebuilt exactly by running `/usr/local/bin/python3 make_data.py`.

Target structure built into the generator:

- shaded pools average about 4.1 ug/cm2, uncovered pools about 6.8 ug/cm2
- spread between pools about 1.3 ug/cm2 (a pool-level offset applied to all five of its rows)
- spread among the five sampling points inside a pool about 0.7 ug/cm2

Realised in the file as written:

| Group | Pools | Rows | Mean chlorophyll (ug/cm2) | SD of rows (ug/cm2) | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shaded | 6 | 30 | 4.05 | 1.38 | 1.63 | 6.59 |
| uncovered | 6 | 30 | 6.89 | 1.38 | 4.01 | 9.37 |

Mean within-pool standard deviation across all 12 pools: 0.70 ug/cm2. Pool means range from 2.56 to
8.63 ug/cm2. Pool surface areas range from 0.24 to 1.32 m2.

There are no missing values: all 60 rows are complete in all five columns.
