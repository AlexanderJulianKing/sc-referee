# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `fermentation_treatment` column of `gari_fermentation_batches.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: total_cyanogenic_potential_mg_hcn_eq_per_kg_dw, ph_72h_ph_units, titratable_acidity_percent_lactic_acid, moisture_content_percent.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
