# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `sub_colony` column of `penguin_chick_condition.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_mass_g, flipper_length_mm, haemoglobin_g_dl, corticosterone_ng_ml.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
