# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `screen_time_group` column of `screen_time_cohort.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: bmi_z_score, waist_circumference_cm, fasting_insulin_miu_l, fasting_triglycerides_mmol_l, hdl_cholesterol_mmol_l, alt_u_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
