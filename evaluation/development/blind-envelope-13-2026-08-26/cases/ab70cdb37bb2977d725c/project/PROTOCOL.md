# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `titration_schedule` column of `gout_titration_outcomes.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: serum_urate_umol_l, gout_flares_past_3_months_count, egfr_ml_min_1_73m2, crp_mg_l, worst_joint_pain_0_10_scale.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
