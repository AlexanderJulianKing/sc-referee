# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `shading_regime` column of `tea_shading_measurements.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: total_catechins_mg_g, caffeine_mg_g, theanine_mg_g, leaf_nitrogen_pct, young_shoot_yield_g.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
