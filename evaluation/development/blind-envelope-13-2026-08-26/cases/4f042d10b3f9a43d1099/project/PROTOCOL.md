# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `drying_method` column of `groundnut_drying_quality.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: moisture_content_percent_wb, aflatoxin_b1_ug_per_kg, free_fatty_acids_percent_oleic.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
