# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `advice_group` column of `ibs_dietary_advice.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: symptom_severity_score_0_500, worst_abdominal_pain_0_10, bloating_days_per_week, stool_consistency_bristol_1_7, quality_of_life_score_0_100.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
