# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `device_group` column of `mad_device_study.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: ahi_events_per_hour, odi_events_per_hour, epworth_sleepiness_score_0_24, min_oxygen_saturation_percent, sleep_efficiency_percent.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
