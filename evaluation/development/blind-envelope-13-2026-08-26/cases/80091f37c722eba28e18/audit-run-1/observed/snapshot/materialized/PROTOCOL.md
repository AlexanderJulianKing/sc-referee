# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `glove_protocol` column of `hand_skin_study.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: transepidermal_water_loss_g_m2_h, stratum_corneum_hydration_au, hand_eczema_severity_score_points, self_reported_itch_score_points, skin_surface_ph, erythema_index_au, hand_symptom_days_last_4_weeks_days.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
