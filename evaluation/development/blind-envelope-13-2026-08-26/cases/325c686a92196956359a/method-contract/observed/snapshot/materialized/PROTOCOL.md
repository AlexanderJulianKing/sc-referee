# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `enrichment_group` column of `elephant_enrichment_welfare.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: stereotypic_behaviour_pct, daily_walking_distance_km, night_recumbent_rest_min, faecal_glucocorticoid_metabolites_ng_per_g, feeding_bout_duration_min, social_proximity_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
