# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `diet_group` column of `ostrich_chick_grower_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_weight_kg, average_daily_gain_g_per_day, feed_conversion_ratio, tibiotarsus_length_cm, hock_circumference_cm, serum_total_protein_g_per_l, serum_calcium_mmol_per_l, packed_cell_volume_percent.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
