# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `midsole_group` column of `trail_midsole_biomechanics.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: ground_contact_time_ms, vertical_oscillation_cm, cadence_steps_per_min, rpe_borg_6_20, finish_time_s.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
