# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `cover_treatment` column of `slurry_store_measurements.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: methane_flux_g_per_m2_per_day, ammonia_flux_gn_per_m2_per_day, nitrous_oxide_flux_mgn_per_m2_per_day, slurry_ph_ph_units, slurry_dry_matter_percent.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
