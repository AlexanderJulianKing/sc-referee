# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `planting_density` column of `cotton_density_plants.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: bolls_per_plant, lint_yield_g, upper_half_mean_length_mm, micronaire, plant_height_cm, first_fruiting_branch_node.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
