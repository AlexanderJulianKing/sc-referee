# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `sown_mix` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: hoverfly_individuals, hoverfly_species_richness, flowering_plant_cover_pct, aphid_colonies_with_hoverfly_larvae, seed_set_seeds_per_head.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
