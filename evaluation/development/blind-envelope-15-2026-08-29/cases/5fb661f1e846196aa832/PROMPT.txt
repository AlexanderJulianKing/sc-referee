SCENARIO

A farmland ecology study of hoverflies on arable field margins. Forty margin strips on commercial farms are the experimental subjects, each strip 100 m long on a separate field, surveyed once in midsummer. Twenty strips were sown with a multi-species flowering mix and twenty with the standard grass-only margin mix; strips were assigned to mixes at establishment three years earlier. Each strip was surveyed by the same protocol on comparable weather days. The five outcome variables below were declared in the survey protocol, in this order, before the survey season.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Hoverfly individuals counted on a fixed 50 m walk. Flower mix strips average roughly 24 individuals, grass-only strips roughly 12, with strip-to-strip spread of about 8.
2. Hoverfly species richness recorded on the strip. Roughly 7.4 and 4.9, spread about 2.0.
3. Flowering plant cover on the strip, as a percentage of ground area. Roughly 34 and 12, spread about 9.
4. Aphid colonies containing hoverfly larvae, per 20 crop plants inspected at the field edge. Roughly 5.2 and 3.2, spread about 2.0.
5. Seed set of sentinel phytometer plants placed on the strip, in seeds per flower head. Roughly 41 and 35, spread about 9.
