# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `drainage_status` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: methane_flux_mgc_m2_h, respiration_co2_flux_mgc_m2_h, water_table_depth_cm, sphagnum_cover_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
