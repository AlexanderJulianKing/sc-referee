# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `nicotine_product` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: exhaled_co_ppm, cigarettes_smoked_cpd, urge_to_smoke_vas_0_100.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
