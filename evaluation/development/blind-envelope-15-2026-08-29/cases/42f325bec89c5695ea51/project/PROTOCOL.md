# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `formulation` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: intraocular_pressure_mmhg, osdi_score_0_100, tear_film_breakup_time_s, conjunctival_hyperaemia_grade_0_3, corneal_staining_score_0_15.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
