# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `supplement_group` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: mass_change_g, resting_metabolic_rate_ml_o2_per_h, faecal_corticosterone_ng_per_g, distance_moved_per_night_m.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
