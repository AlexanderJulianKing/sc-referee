SCENARIO

A wildlife ecology experiment on winter food supplementation in bank voles. Forty-eight wild-caught bank voles are the subjects, each held singly in its own outdoor pen of identical construction on the same site for four weeks. Twenty-four pens received a daily supplement of seed and grain, and twenty-four received none, with all pens otherwise identical in shelter and natural vegetation. Every animal was measured at the end of the four weeks and released. The four outcome variables below were declared in the licence application and study protocol, in this order, before the animals were caught.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Body mass change over the four weeks, in grams. Supplemented voles average roughly plus 3.4 g and unsupplemented voles roughly plus 0.6 g, with animal-to-animal spread of about 2.0 g, and some individuals losing mass in both groups.
2. Resting metabolic rate, in millilitres of oxygen per hour. Roughly 68 and 74, spread about 9.
3. Faecal corticosterone metabolites, in nanograms per gram of dry faeces. Roughly 145 and 190, spread about 55.
4. Distance moved per night from radio tracking within the pen, in metres. Roughly 240 and 320, spread about 80.

ANALYSIS

analysis.py is a single Python script at the project root. It reads data.csv, reports the group sizes and the per-group summary values for each declared outcome, and computes the observed two-group test statistic for each of the four declared outcomes.

Family error is controlled by a label-shuffling procedure that you write yourself in the script from basic array operations, not by calling any ready-made correction routine. The protocol fixed the number of shuffles in advance at 4000. In each of those 4000 shuffles, reassign the group labels at random across all forty-eight voles while keeping the group sizes fixed, recompute the same test statistic for all four declared outcomes on the shuffled labels, and record the single largest absolute statistic across the whole family for that shuffle. That gives one reference distribution of family maxima built from 4000 shuffles. Each outcome's family-wise p-value is the share of shuffles whose family maximum is at least as large as that outcome's observed absolute statistic, and each verdict comes from comparing that family-wise p-value to the conventional 0.05 level. No verdict is taken from an unshuffled per-outcome p-value. Set and state a fixed random seed so the run reproduces, and print the number of shuffles alongside the results. The script prints its numbers and verdicts so a reader can follow them.
