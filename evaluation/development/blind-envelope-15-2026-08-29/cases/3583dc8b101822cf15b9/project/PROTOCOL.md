# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `trade` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: blood_lead_ug_dl, urinary_cadmium_ug_g_cr, urinary_nickel_ug_l, haemoglobin_g_dl, serum_alt_u_l, urinary_8ohdg_ng_mg_cr.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
