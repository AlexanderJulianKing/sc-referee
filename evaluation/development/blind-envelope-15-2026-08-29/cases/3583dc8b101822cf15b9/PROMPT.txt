SCENARIO

An environmental health survey of informal electronic waste recycling. Ninety-six adult male workers from one city are the subjects: forty-eight who dismantle and burn electronic scrap, and forty-eight from a nearby textile trade with no metal exposure, matched on age band and years of work. Each worker gave one blood sample and one spot urine sample on the same morning. Before any sample was analysed, the study statistician split the workers into a discovery half and a validation half by a fixed random allocation, balanced so that each half holds twenty-four exposed and twenty-four control workers, and that allocation was recorded in the data file. The six outcome variables below were declared in the survey protocol, in this order, before the split.

6. Urinary 8-hydroxy-2-deoxyguanosine, in nanograms per milligram of creatinine. Roughly 8.4 and 5.7, spread about 3.0.
