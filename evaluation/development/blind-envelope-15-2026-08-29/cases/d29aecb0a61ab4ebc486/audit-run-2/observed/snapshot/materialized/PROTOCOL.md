# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `drying_cycle` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: cooking_loss_pct, optimal_cooking_time_min, firmness_n, colour_b_star, furosine_mg_100g_protein.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
