# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `planting_depth` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: flower_count, stigma_yield_mg, daughter_corm_mass_g, time_to_first_flower_d.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
