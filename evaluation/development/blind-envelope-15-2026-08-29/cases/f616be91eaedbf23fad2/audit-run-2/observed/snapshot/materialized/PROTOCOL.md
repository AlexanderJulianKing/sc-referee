# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `coagulant` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: yield_g_per_100g, hardness_n, syneresis_pct, whiteness_index, protein_g_per_100g, ph.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
