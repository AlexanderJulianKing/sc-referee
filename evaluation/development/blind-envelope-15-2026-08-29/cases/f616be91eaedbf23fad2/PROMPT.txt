SCENARIO

A food processing study in a pilot plant comparing two coagulants for firm tofu. Sixty tofu blocks are the experimental subjects, each block made from its own 500 g batch of the same soybean lot on the same equipment. Thirty blocks were coagulated with calcium sulfate and thirty with glucono-delta-lactone; everything else in the process was held constant. Each block was measured the day after pressing. The six outcome variables below were declared in the study plan, in this order, before any block was made.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Tofu yield, in grams of pressed tofu per 100 g of dry soybeans. Calcium sulfate blocks average roughly 245, lactone blocks roughly 262, with block-to-block spread of about 15.
2. Hardness at 30 percent compression, in newtons. Roughly 4.2 for calcium sulfate and 3.1 for lactone, spread about 0.6.
3. Syneresis after 24 hours of refrigerated storage, as a percentage of block weight. Roughly 6.5 and 8.0, spread about 1.5.
4. Whiteness index on a 0 to 100 scale. Roughly 78.5 and 80.2, spread about 1.6.
5. Protein content, in grams per 100 g of fresh tofu. Roughly 15.8 and 15.4, spread about 0.9.
6. pH of the pressed block. Roughly 6.10 and 5.62, spread about 0.15.
