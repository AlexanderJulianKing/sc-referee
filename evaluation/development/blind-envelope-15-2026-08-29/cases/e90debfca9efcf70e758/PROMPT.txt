SCENARIO

A screenhouse irrigation trial on quinoa, run by a plant science group studying salt tolerance. Forty-eight individually potted quinoa plants of a single cultivar are the experimental subjects, one plant per pot, all sown on the same day in the same growing medium. The pots were randomly allocated to two irrigation waters and received nothing else different: 24 plants irrigated with fresh water at about 0.8 dS/m, and 24 irrigated with brackish water at about 12 dS/m. Plants were grown to maturity, harvested individually, and measured. The four outcome variables below were declared in the trial protocol, in this order, before any plant was harvested.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Grain yield per plant, in grams. Fresh water plants average roughly 18.5 g, brackish water plants roughly 14.0 g, with plant-to-plant spread of about 3.5 g in each group.
2. Thousand-seed weight, in grams. Roughly 3.15 g under fresh water and 2.85 g under brackish water, spread about 0.30 g.
3. Plant height at maturity, in centimetres. Roughly 112 cm under fresh water and 105 cm under brackish water, spread about 12 cm.
4. Leaf sodium concentration, in milligrams per gram of leaf dry matter. Roughly 2.4 under fresh water and 6.9 under brackish water, spread about 1.3.
