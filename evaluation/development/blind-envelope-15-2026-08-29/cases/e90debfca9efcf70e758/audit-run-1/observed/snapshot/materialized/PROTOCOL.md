# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `irrigation_water` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: grain_yield_g, thousand_seed_weight_g, plant_height_cm, leaf_sodium_mg_g.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
