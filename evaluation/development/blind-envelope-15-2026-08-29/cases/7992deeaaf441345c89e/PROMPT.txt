SCENARIO

A veterinary husbandry trial on ultraviolet lighting for captive bred juvenile bearded dragons. Forty juveniles of the same hatch cohort are the subjects, each housed in its own vivarium of identical size, diet and temperature gradient for twelve weeks. Twenty were kept under a compact fluorescent ultraviolet B lamp and twenty under a linear T5 high output ultraviolet B lamp, with lamps at the manufacturer's stated distance and replaced on schedule. Every animal was measured at the end of week twelve. The five outcome variables below were declared in the trial protocol, in this order, before the animals were allocated.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Plasma 25-hydroxyvitamin D3, in nanomoles per litre. T5 animals average roughly 155 and compact fluorescent animals roughly 98, with animal-to-animal spread of about 40.
2. Plasma ionised calcium, in millimoles per litre. Roughly 1.52 and 1.44, spread about 0.10.
3. Body mass gain over twelve weeks, in grams. Roughly 68 and 59, spread about 14.
4. Snout to vent length gain over twelve weeks, in millimetres. Roughly 41 and 37, spread about 6.
5. Radiographic humeral cortical thickness ratio, a unitless ratio of cortical to total bone width. Roughly 0.34 and 0.30, spread about 0.05.
