# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `lamp_type` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: plasma_25ohd3_nmol_l, plasma_ionised_calcium_mmol_l, body_mass_gain_g, snout_vent_length_gain_mm, humeral_cortical_thickness_ratio.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
