# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `acetazolamide_group` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: spo2_pct, lake_louise_ams_score, resting_heart_rate_bpm, periodic_breathing_events_per_hr.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
