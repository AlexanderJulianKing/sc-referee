# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `hangboard_protocol` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: peak_force_n, critical_force_pct, time_to_failure_s, rate_of_force_development_n_per_s, resaturation_half_time_s, moves_to_failure, finger_soreness_0_10.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
