# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `ventilation` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: tvoc_mg_m3, urinary_acetone_mg_l, eye_irritation_0_10, headache_0_10, neurobehavioural_score_0_30.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
