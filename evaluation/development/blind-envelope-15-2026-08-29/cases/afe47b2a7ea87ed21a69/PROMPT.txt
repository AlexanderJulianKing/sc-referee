SCENARIO

An occupational hygiene survey of nail salon technicians and solvent vapour exposure. Fifty-six technicians are the subjects, each working in a different salon, each monitored across one full working shift of similar length and client load. Twenty-eight technicians work at benches fitted with source-capture local exhaust ventilation, and twenty-eight work at benches with no source capture and general room ventilation only. Each technician wore a personal sampler for the shift, gave an end-of-shift urine sample, and completed the symptom items at the end of the shift. The five outcome variables below were declared in the survey protocol, in this order, before fieldwork began.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Personal airborne total volatile organic compounds over the shift, in milligrams per cubic metre. Captured benches average roughly 1.4, uncaptured benches roughly 3.3, with technician-to-technician spread of about 0.9.
2. End-of-shift urinary acetone, in milligrams per litre. Roughly 1.8 and 3.5, spread about 1.1.
3. End-of-shift eye irritation on a 0 to 10 rating scale. Roughly 2.1 and 3.4, spread about 1.5.
4. End-of-shift headache on a 0 to 10 rating scale. Roughly 1.8 and 2.5, spread about 1.4.
5. Neurobehavioural symptom questionnaire score, 0 to 30 points. Roughly 6.5 and 8.0, spread about 4.0.
