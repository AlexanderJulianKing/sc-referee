SCENARIO

Sixty age-synchronised assay plates are the experimental subjects, each plate seeded and scored on its own, with the plate value being the average of the worms scored on that plate. Thirty plates were exposed from the first larval stage to 100 micromolar of a candidate neuroactive agrochemical in the growth medium, and thirty control plates received the solvent carrier only. Nothing else differed between plates. The eight outcome variables below were declared in the screen protocol, in this order, before scoring began.
