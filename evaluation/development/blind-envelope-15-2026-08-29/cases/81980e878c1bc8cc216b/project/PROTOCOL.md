# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `exposure` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: mean_lifespan_d, total_brood_size_eggs, pumping_rate_pumps_per_min, thrashing_rate_bends_per_min, body_length_um, age_at_first_egg_h, defecation_interval_s, crawling_speed_um_per_s.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
