# Karhusuo peatland chamber survey

The Karhusuo peat complex has twelve permanent monitoring plots: six in
the sector that was drained by ditches decades ago, and six in the
sector where the ditches were blocked and the water table was allowed to
recover. Each plot has a fixed steel collar set into the peat.

During the 2024 growing season a field crew visited every plot five
times. On each visit a static closed chamber was seated on the plot
collar, the headspace was sampled over half an hour, and the resulting
methane flux was written down together with the air temperature inside
the chamber. Twelve plots visited five times each gives 60 lines in the
table.

Columns:

- plot_id: label of the permanent monitoring plot (DR-01 to DR-06 in the
  drained sector, RW-01 to RW-06 in the rewetted sector); the same plot
  reappears once per survey round
- water_table_regime: which sector the plot belongs to, drained or
  rewetted
- survey_round: which of the five seasonal visits the reading comes from
  (1 = early May through 5 = late September)
- chamber_temp_c: air temperature inside the chamber during the
  measurement, in degrees Celsius
- ch4_flux_mg_m2_h: methane flux for that chamber deployment, in
  milligrams of CH4 per square metre per hour

One row is: one static-chamber methane flux measurement taken on one monitoring plot during one of the five seasonal survey rounds
Independent unit column: plot_id
