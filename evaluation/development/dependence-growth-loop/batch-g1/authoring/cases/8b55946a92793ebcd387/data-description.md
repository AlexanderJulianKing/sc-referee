# Kilda Sound kelp growline seeding-density trial

Eight suspended kelp growlines were deployed side by side in Kilda Sound at the
start of the same growing season. Four growlines were seeded at the sparse
density and four at the dense density; the density is fixed for a whole
growline. After twelve weeks, divers sampled five blades from five fixed slots
along each growline and recorded how fast each sampled blade had been
elongating.

One row is: one sampled kelp blade taken from one growline, with its measured elongation rate
Independent unit column: growline_id

Columns:
- growline_id: the growline the blade was taken from (L01 through L08)
- seeding_density: the seeding treatment applied to that entire growline, either sparse or dense
- blade_slot: which of the five fixed sampling positions along the growline the blade came from (1 to 5)
- elongation_cm_per_day: the blade's elongation rate in centimetres per day

Every growline contributes five rows, one per sampled blade, so the same
growline appears repeatedly in the file. Blades from one growline shared the
same rope, the same mooring depth and the same water column for the whole
season.
