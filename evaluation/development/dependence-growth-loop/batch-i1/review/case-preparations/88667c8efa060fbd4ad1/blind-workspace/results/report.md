# Water-table drawdown and methane efflux in a boreal fen

## Design and unit of analysis

Twelve permanent chamber collars were installed in a boreal fen: six in intact peat and six inside an experimental drainage block. Each collar was revisited in three seasonal flux sessions, so the source table holds 36 measurement rows for 12 collars.

The three sessions recorded for a collar are repeated measurements of the same physical collar; they share its peat profile, vegetation and microtopography and are not independent replicates of the drainage treatment. Each collar is therefore reduced to one analysed value, the mean of its session fluxes, before any test is applied. The independent unit is the collar, so the comparison rests on 12 values, not 36.

## Group summary (collar means)

- intact: n = 6 collars, mean of collar means = 14.00, median = 13.80, mean water table = 6.06 cm
- drained: n = 6 collars, mean of collar means = 9.10, median = 8.70, mean water table = 34.61 cm
- intact minus drained: 4.90 mg m^-2 h^-1 in means, 5.10 mg m^-2 h^-1 in medians

## Test

A two-sided exact Mann-Whitney U test was run on the 12 collar-level mean fluxes, six per treatment, with the collar as the independent unit. The exact null distribution is used because the group sizes are small and no collar mean is tied.

U = 35, exact two-sided p = 0.004329, common-language effect size = 0.972 (the probability that a randomly chosen intact collar exceeds a randomly chosen drained collar).

[selected-result] Collar-mean CH4 flux was higher in intact fen than in drained fen (exact two-sided Mann-Whitney U = 35, n = 6 vs 6 collars, p = 0.004329; group means 14.00 vs 9.10 mg m^-2 h^-1, difference 4.90).

## Notes

Session-to-session variation within a collar is small (ranges of 0.90 to 1.70 mg m^-2 h^-1) next to the spread between collars, so averaging the three sessions loses little information while keeping one row per independent unit in the test. Water-table depth is reported as a manipulation check and was not used in the test.
