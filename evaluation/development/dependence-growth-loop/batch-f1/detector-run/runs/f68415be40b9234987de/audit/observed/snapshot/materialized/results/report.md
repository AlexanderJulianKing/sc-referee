# Methane efflux from drained versus rewetted peat collars

## Design

Twenty-four gas-flux collars were installed, one collar per independently
selected raised-bog basin, and every collar contributed exactly one midsummer
chamber closure. The analysed table holds 24 rows and 24 distinct collars, so
each number entering the test comes from a different independent unit.

| management | collars | mean CH4 flux (mg m^-2 h^-1) | SD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| drained | 12 | 6.00 | 3.16 |
| restored | 12 | 18.00 | 4.69 |

## Test

Welch's two-sample t-test (unequal variances, two-sided) comparing rewetted
with drained collars, one observation per collar.

- mean difference (restored minus drained): 12.00 mg m^-2 h^-1
- 95% confidence interval for the difference: 8.59 to 15.41 mg m^-2 h^-1
- Welch t = 7.35 on df = 19.29
- p < 0.0001
- Cohen's d (pooled SD = 4.00): 3.00

[selected-result] Rewetted peat collars emitted substantially more methane than drained collars: mean difference 12.00 mg m^-2 h^-1 (95% CI 8.59 to 15.41), Welch t(19.29) = 7.35, p < 0.0001, with 12 collars per group and one flux measurement per collar.

## Notes

No collar contributes more than one row, the two groups are balanced at 12
collars each, and the independence assumption of the t-test is satisfied by
the sampling design rather than by any post hoc averaging.
