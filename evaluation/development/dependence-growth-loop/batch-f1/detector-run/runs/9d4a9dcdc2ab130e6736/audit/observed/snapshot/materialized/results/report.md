# Deficit irrigation and leaf stomatal conductance (Serra Alta olive trial)

## Sampling

Steady-state porometer readings of leaf stomatal conductance (g_sw, mmol m^-2 s^-1)
were taken mid-morning in the third week of the stress period. 96 sunlit leaves were
logged, 8 per tree, across 12 trees split between the two irrigation regimes.

| irrigation | leaves | mean g_sw | SD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| full | 48 | 300.00 | 30.75 |
| deficit | 48 | 276.00 | 27.43 |

## Test

Two-sided two-sample Student t-test (pooled variance) on the leaf readings,
full irrigation versus deficit irrigation.

- mean difference (full - deficit): 24.00 mmol m^-2 s^-1
- t = 4.035, df = 94, p < 0.001

[selected-result] Leaves under full irrigation had higher stomatal conductance than leaves under deficit irrigation (300.00 vs 276.00 mmol m^-2 s^-1, difference 24.00; two-sample t-test t = 4.035, df = 94, p < 0.001).
