# Hooked-stick preference in juvenile New Caledonian crows

## Trials

24 two-option tool-choice trials were recorded from 6 hand-raised juvenile crows.
Each trial was scored 1 when the bird lifted the hooked stick and 0 when it lifted
the straight stick.

## Per-bird tally

| bird_id | trials | hooked choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NC01 | 4 | 4 |
| NC02 | 4 | 4 |
| NC03 | 4 | 3 |
| NC04 | 4 | 4 |
| NC05 | 4 | 2 |
| NC06 | 4 | 1 |

## Test

Every recorded trial was entered as one observation and the pooled outcomes were
compared with a chance rate of 0.500 using an exact two-sided binomial test.

- Hooked choices: 18 of 24
- Observed proportion: 0.7500
- Exact two-sided p-value: 0.022656

## Conclusion

[selected-result] Juvenile New Caledonian crows chose the hooked stick on 18 of 24 trials (75.00%), and an exact two-sided binomial test against a chance rate of 0.500 rejects chance responding at the 5% level (p = 0.022656).
