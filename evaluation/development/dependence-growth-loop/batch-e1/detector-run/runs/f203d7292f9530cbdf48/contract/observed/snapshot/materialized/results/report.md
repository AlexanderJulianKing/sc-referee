# Pelleted vs. bare seed: emergence in single-seed pots

## Data

24 pots, one seed and one maternal plant per pot, each pot scored once for
seedling emergence at day 28. Each pot is an independent experimental unit and
contributes exactly one row.

| Seed treatment | Emerged | Not emerged | Pots | Emergence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pelleted | 9 | 3 | 12 | 75.0% |
| bare | 3 | 9 | 12 | 25.0% |

## Analysis

Fisher's exact test (two-sided) on the 2x2 table of seed treatment by
emergence at day 28, with one Bernoulli outcome per independent pot.

## Result

Sample odds ratio (pelleted vs. bare) = 9.00, two-sided p = 0.0391.
Emergence was 50.0 percentage points higher in pelleted pots.

[selected-result] Two-sided Fisher's exact test on 24 independent single-seed pots: emergence 9/12 (75.0%) with pelleted seed vs. 3/12 (25.0%) with bare seed, sample odds ratio 9.00, p = 0.0391; the difference is statistically significant at alpha = 0.05.
