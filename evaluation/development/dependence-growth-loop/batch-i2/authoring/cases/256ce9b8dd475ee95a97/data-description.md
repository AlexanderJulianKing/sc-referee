# Mason bee homing trips at a rotated nest block

Eight individually tagged mason bee (Osmia) females were filmed at a shared
nesting block after the block was turned on its mount, so the familiar landmark
cues no longer lined up with the tube openings. Each time a female came back
from a foraging run, observers wrote down whether she finally entered her own
(natal) tube or a different tube, how long she hovered at the block face before
committing, and the air temperature at that moment. Females differed in how
busy they were during the observation session, so most females appear in
several lines of the table.

Columns:

- bee_tag: paint-and-number tag of the female making the trip (B-01 through B-08)
- trip_index: order of that trip within the same female's session, starting at 1
- ambient_temp_c: air temperature at the block face, degrees Celsius
- search_time_s: seconds spent hovering in front of the block before entering a tube
- natal_tube_return: 1 if she entered her own tube, 0 if she entered another tube

One row is: one provisioning trip by one tagged female bee, scored for whether she returned to her own nest tube
Independent unit column: bee_tag
One trial is: one row
