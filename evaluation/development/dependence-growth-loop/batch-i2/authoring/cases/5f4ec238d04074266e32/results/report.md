# Sourdough starter acidity: rye vs. wheat refresh flour

## Data

48 aliquots were titrated after a 24 h refresh; the response is titratable acidity in
mL of 0.1 M NaOH to the phenolphthalein endpoint.

| Refresh flour | Aliquots | Mean acidity (mL) | SD (mL) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rye | 24 | 10.000 | 0.404 |
| Wheat | 24 | 8.600 | 0.349 |

## Analysis

Welch's two-sample t-test (two-sided, unequal variances) comparing the acidity of every
rye aliquot with the acidity of every wheat aliquot.

## Result

Mean difference (rye - wheat) = 1.400 mL; t = 12.84, df = 45.0, p < 0.0001.

[selected-result] Welch's two-sample t-test on 48 aliquots shows that rye-refreshed starters reach a higher titratable acidity than wheat-refreshed starters, by 1.400 mL of 0.1 M NaOH (t = 12.84, df = 45.0, p < 0.0001).
