# Vineyard soil CO2 efflux under biochar amendment

This table comes from a field trial in a hillside vineyard block. Twelve
inter-row plots were laid out before the growing season: six had biochar worked
into the top 15 cm of soil, and six were left unamended as controls. A permanent
collar was installed in each plot, and a static chamber was seated on that collar
during five sampling campaigns spread from early spring to late summer.

Each visit produced one soil CO2 efflux reading for that plot, recorded together
with the soil temperature at 10 cm depth and the volumetric water content
measured at the same moment. Because every plot was revisited in all five
campaigns, the same twelve plots each appear five times in the table, and the
five readings from a single plot describe the same patch of ground rather than
five separate patches.

Columns:
- plot_id: label of the vineyard plot the collar sits in (PLOT-01 through PLOT-12)
- treatment: biochar or control, fixed for the whole season
- campaign: sampling campaign number, 1 (earliest) through 5 (latest)
- co2_flux_umol_m2_s: soil CO2 efflux, micromoles of CO2 per square meter per second
- soil_temp_10cm_c: soil temperature at 10 cm depth, degrees Celsius
- vwc_pct: volumetric soil water content in the top 10 cm, percent

One row is: one static-chamber CO2 efflux reading taken from one vineyard plot during one sampling campaign
Independent unit column: plot_id
