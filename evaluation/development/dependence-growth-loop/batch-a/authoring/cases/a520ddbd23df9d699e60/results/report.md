# Cinder Flats seed-coating trial: third-season cover target

## Design

24 abandoned dryland plots on the Cinder Flats terrace were each sown
once, with the seed lot randomly assigned per plot, and each plot was scored
once at the end of the third growing season. A plot contributes exactly one
row, so the units entering the test are independent of one another.

## Attainment of the 40.0% perennial-cover target

| Treatment | Plots | Met target | Below target | Share meeting target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inoculant-coated seed | 12 | 9 | 3 | 75.0% |
| Uncoated seed | 12 | 3 | 9 | 25.0% |

## Cover readings

| Treatment | Mean cover (%) | Median cover (%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inoculant-coated seed | 45.82 | 45.55 |
| Uncoated seed | 32.50 | 31.65 |

## Test and result

The two seed treatments were compared with a two-sided Fisher's exact test
on the 2x2 table of seed treatment by target attainment. The gap in
attainment share is 50.0 percentage points in favor of the coated seed.

[selected-result] Two-sided Fisher's exact test on 24 independent plots (one row per plot): 9 of 12 inoculant-coated plots versus 3 of 12 uncoated plots reached the 40.0% perennial-cover target; odds ratio 9.00, p = 0.0391, significant at alpha = 0.05.

The exact test conditions on the observed margins and assumes only that the
24 plots are independent, which the one-row-per-plot design supplies.
