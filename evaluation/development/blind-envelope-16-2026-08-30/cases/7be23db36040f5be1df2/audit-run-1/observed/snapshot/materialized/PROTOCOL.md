# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `cold_schedule` column of `molar_cold_therapy.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: swelling_d2_mm, opening_d2_mm, pain_d1_vas, pain_d3_vas, rescue_tabs_n, diet_return_d.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
