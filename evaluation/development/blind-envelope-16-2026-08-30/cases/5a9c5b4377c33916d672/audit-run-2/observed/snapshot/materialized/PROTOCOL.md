# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `substrate_depth` column of `green_roof_survey.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: plant_richness_count, veg_cover_pct, substrate_moisture_pct, temp_reduction_c, invert_abundance_count.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
