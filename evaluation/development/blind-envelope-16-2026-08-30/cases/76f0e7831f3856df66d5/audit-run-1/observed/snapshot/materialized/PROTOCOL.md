# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `cut_regime` column of `hedge_sections.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: berry_mass_gpm, stem_density_spm, plant_richness_spp, basal_gap_pct, invert_biomass_mgpm.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
