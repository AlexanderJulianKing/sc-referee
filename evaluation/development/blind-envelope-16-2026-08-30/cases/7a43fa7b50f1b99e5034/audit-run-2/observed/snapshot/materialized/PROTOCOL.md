# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `floor_type` column of `quail_flooring.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_weight_g, feed_intake_g_d, footpad_score_pts, tibia_strength_n, corticosterone_ng_ml, tonic_immobility_s.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
