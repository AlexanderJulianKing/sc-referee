# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `frying_method` column of `plantain_frying_batches.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: oil_content_g100g, acrylamide_ug_kg, breaking_force_n, colour_b_cielab, crispness_score_pts.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
