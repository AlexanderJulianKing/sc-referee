# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `catchment` column of `eel_catchment_survey.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: hg_mg_kg, pcb6_ug_kg, erod_pmol_min_mg, fulton_k, hsi_pct, lipid_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
