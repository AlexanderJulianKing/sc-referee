# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `dough_line` column of `bakery_flour_dust.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: dust_mg_m3, fev1_drop_ml, ige_wheat_ku_l, nasal_symptom_pts.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
