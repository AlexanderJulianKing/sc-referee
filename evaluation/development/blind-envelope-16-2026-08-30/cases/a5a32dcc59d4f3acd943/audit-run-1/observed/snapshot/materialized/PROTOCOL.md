# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `noise_type` column of `tinnitus_sound_therapy.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: thi_pts, loudness_pts, sleep_idx_pts, anxiety_pts, mml_db.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
