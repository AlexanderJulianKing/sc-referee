# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `landscape` column of `hares.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_mass_kg, hind_foot_mm, cortisol_ng_g, haemoglobin_g_dl, egg_count_epg.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
