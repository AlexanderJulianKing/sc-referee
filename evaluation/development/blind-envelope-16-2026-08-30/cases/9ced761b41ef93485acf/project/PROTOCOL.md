# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `inoculation` column of `chickpea_inoculation.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: shoot_dw_g, root_dw_g, nodule_no, nodule_dw_mg, shoot_n_pct, pod_no, seed_yield_g.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
