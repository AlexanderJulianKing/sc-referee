# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `programme` column of `swimmers.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: sprint_50_free_s, tethered_force_n, cmj_height_cm, shoulder_ir_torque_nm.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
