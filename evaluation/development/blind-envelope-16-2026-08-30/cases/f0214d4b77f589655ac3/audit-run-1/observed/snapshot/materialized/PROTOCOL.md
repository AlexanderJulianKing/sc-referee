# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `girth_type` column of `girth_pad_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: lesion_score_pts, hair_loss_cm2, nociceptive_threshold_n, body_condition_pts, rectal_temp_c.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
