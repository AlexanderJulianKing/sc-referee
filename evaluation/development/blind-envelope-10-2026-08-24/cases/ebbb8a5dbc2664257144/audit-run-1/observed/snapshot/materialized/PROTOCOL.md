# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `liner_group` column of `heat_strain.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: peak_core_temp_c, peak_heart_rate_bpm, sweat_loss_l, exhaustion_time_min.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
