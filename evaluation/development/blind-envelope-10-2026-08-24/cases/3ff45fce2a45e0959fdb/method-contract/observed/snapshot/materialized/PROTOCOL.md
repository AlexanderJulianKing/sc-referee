# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `dressing_group` column of `venous_ulcer_dressings.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: area_reduction_pct, pain_vas_mm, exudate_score, periwound_erythema_mm, days_to_half_healing, wound_qol_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
