# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `site_group` column of `air_quality_winter.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: pm25_ug_m3, pm10_ug_m3, no2_ug_m3, o3_ug_m3, black_carbon_ug_m3.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
