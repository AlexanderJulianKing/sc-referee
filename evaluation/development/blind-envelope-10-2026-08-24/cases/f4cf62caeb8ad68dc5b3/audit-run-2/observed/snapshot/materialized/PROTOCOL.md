# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `disturbance_group` column of `marmots.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_mass_kg, fgm_ng_per_g, emergence_doy, vigilance_pct, ectoparasite_count.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
