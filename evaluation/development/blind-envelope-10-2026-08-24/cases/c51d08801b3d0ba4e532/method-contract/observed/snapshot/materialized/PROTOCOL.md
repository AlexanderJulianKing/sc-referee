# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `pool_system` column of `lifeguard_airway.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: feno_ppb, fev1_pct_pred, fvc_pct_pred, airway_symptom_score, eye_irritation_score, cc16_ug_l, cough_days_per_month.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
