# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `supplement_group` column of `camel_milk_outcomes.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: milk_yield_l_per_day, milk_fat_pct, milk_protein_pct, body_condition_score, plasma_glucose_mmol_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
