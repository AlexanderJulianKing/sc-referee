# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `conche_group` column of `chocolate_batches.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: particle_d90_um, hardness_n, melt_peak_c, gloss_gu, bitterness_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
