# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `program_group` column of `pulmonary_rehab_outcomes.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: six_min_walk_m, cat_score, quad_torque_nm, sit_to_stand_reps.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
