# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `harvest_group` column of `hemp_harvest_timing.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: bast_fibre_yield_g, tensile_strength_mpa, stem_diameter_mm, cbd_pct_dry, stem_moisture_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
