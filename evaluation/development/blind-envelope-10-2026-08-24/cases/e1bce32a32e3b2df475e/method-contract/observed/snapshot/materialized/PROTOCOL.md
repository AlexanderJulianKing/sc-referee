# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `exo_group` column of `exo_picking_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: peak_lumbar_compression_n, borg_exertion_score, round_time_min, picking_errors, shoulder_discomfort_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
