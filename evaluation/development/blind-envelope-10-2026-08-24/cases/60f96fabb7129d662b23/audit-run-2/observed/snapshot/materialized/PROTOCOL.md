# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `habitat_group` column of `fox_habitat_measurements.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_condition_index, home_range_km2, faecal_cortisol_ng_per_g, diet_shannon_index.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
