# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `plasma_group` column of `almond_plasma_lots.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: surrogate_log_reduction, peroxide_value_meq_kg, colour_l_star, moisture_pct, rancid_odour_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
