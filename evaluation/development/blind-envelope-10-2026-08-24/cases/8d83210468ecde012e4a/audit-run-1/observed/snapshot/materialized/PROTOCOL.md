# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `program_group` column of `wheat_fungicide_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: grain_yield_g, tgw_g, septoria_severity_pct, green_canopy_days, plant_height_cm, spike_count.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
