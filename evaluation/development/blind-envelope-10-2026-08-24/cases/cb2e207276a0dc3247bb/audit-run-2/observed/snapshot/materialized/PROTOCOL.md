# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `n_rate_group` column of `sorghum_nitrogen_plants.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: grain_yield_g, panicle_length_cm, stem_brix_pct, plant_height_cm.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
