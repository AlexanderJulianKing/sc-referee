# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `rnfl_thickness_um` between the two levels of `drop_regimen` (`timolol` and `latanoprost`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the patient. The column `patient_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each patient has one distinct `patient_id` value. Each patient was randomised to one drop regimen and stayed in it for the whole year, so the patient is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `rnfl_thickness_um` between the two levels of `drop_regimen`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
