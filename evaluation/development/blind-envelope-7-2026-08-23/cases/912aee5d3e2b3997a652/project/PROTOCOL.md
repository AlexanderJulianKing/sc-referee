# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `clean_root_yield_t_ha` between the two levels of `seed_treatment` (`standard` and `biological`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the field. The column `field_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each field has one distinct `field_id` value. Each field was drilled with one of the two seed treatments and harvested whole, so the field is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `clean_root_yield_t_ha` between the two levels of `seed_treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
