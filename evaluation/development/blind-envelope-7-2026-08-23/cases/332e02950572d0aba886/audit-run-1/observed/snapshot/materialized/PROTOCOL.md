# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `body_weight_g` between the two levels of `feed_treatment` (`standard` and `supplemented`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the grow-out pond. The column `pond_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each pond has one distinct `pond_id` value. The feed treatment was assigned at the pond level and fed to the whole pond for the grow-out, so the pond is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `body_weight_g` between the two levels of `feed_treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
