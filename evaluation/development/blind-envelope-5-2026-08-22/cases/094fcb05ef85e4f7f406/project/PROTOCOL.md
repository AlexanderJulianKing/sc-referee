# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `herbage_kg_dm_ha` between the two levels of `rotation` (`fast_rotation` and `set_stocking`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the paddock. The column `paddock_name` carries the value that
identifies it: each paddock has one distinct `paddock_name` value. The rotation was assigned to the whole fenced paddock, so the paddock is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `herbage_kg_dm_ha` between the two levels of `rotation`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
