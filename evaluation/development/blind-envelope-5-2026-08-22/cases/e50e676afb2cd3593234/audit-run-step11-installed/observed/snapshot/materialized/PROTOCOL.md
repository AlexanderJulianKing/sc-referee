# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `peak_glucose_mmol_l` between the two levels of `breakfast_arm` (`refined_cereal` and `high_protein`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the volunteer. The column `volunteer_code` carries the value that
identifies it: each volunteer has one distinct `volunteer_code` value. Volunteers were randomised as whole people and kept their arm for all 14 mornings, so the volunteer is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `peak_glucose_mmol_l` between the two levels of `breakfast_arm`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
