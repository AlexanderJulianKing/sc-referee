# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `stomatal_conductance_mmol_m2_s` between the two levels of `canopy_treatment` (`shade_trees` and `full_sun`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the coffee shrub. The column `shrub_label` carries the value that
identifies it: each shrub has one distinct `shrub_label` value. The canopy treatment is a property of the shrub rather than of the leaf, so the shrub is the unit that carries a group label.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `stomatal_conductance_mmol_m2_s` between the two levels of `canopy_treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
