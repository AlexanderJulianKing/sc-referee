# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `discrimination_index` between the two levels of `housing` (`enriched` and `standard`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the rat. The column `rat_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each rat has one distinct `rat_id` value. Housing was assigned to whole animals, so the rat is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `discrimination_index` between the two levels of `housing`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
