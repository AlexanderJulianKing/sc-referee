# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `probing_depth_mm` between the two levels of `paste_arm` (`stannous_fluoride` and `sodium_fluoride`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the patient. The column `patient_code` carries the value that
identifies it: each patient has one distinct `patient_code` value. Patients were randomised as whole people and the arm is a property of the patient rather than of the tooth, so the patient is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `probing_depth_mm` between the two levels of `paste_arm`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
