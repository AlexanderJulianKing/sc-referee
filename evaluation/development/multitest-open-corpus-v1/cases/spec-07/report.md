# Bacillus probiotic in creep feed for weaned piglets

Half of one weaning batch (48 piglets) received a Bacillus-based probiotic in the creep
feed for 28 days; the other 48 stayed on the same basal feed without the additive. Pens
were fed individually, so each piglet counts as one observation. Five outcomes were
recorded: average daily gain, feed conversion ratio, days with scours, faecal
lactobacilli, and serum IgA. Groups were compared with Welch two-sample t-tests at the
conventional 5% cutoff.

| Outcome | Basal | Probiotic | p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Average daily gain (g/d) | 349.5 | 367.1 | 0.1331 | not significant |
| Feed conversion (feed:gain) | 1.69 | 1.59 | 0.0081 | significant |
| Days with scours (of 28) | 3.02 | 2.02 | 0.0246 | significant |
| Faecal lactobacilli (log10 CFU/g) | 7.37 | 8.05 | <0.0001 | significant |
| Serum IgA (mg/L) | 122.2 | 143.3 | 0.0037 | significant |

Four of the five outcomes were significant. The clearest effect is on the gut flora
itself: faecal lactobacilli were about 0.7 log10 CFU/g higher on the probiotic, which is
what the additive is meant to do and confirms the product survived the feed and the gut.
Feed conversion improved by 0.10 units of feed per unit of gain, scours fell by about one
day per piglet over the 28 days, and serum IgA was about 21 mg/L higher, consistent with
a mild immune effect. Average daily gain was 17.6 g/d higher on the probiotic but did not
reach significance, so growth rate on its own is not something this batch demonstrates.

Whether the additive pays depends mostly on the feed conversion result rather than on
gain. At 1.69 against 1.59, a piglet putting on 10 kg over the period eats roughly 1 kg
less feed, and the fall in scours days should also reduce medication and labour. If the
additive costs less than the value of that saved kilogram of feed per piglet, it is worth
using. We do not have the unit's current inclusion cost in front of us, so that
comparison is left to the feed budget rather than asserted here. A second batch would be
worth running before committing the whole site, ideally powered to say something about daily
gain, which is the outcome the buyer will ask about first.
