# Sawdust versus woven plastic mulch in mature highbush blueberry

## Setting

78 bushes in a mature highbush blueberry planting, 39 under sawdust mulch and 39 under
woven plastic, measured over one season with the individual bush as the unit. Six outcomes
were recorded: marketable yield, mean berry weight, soluble solids, root-zone soil
moisture, weed biomass, and root-zone soil pH.

## Analysis

Each outcome was compared between mulches with a two-sample t-test for a difference in
means and each p-value read against 0.05.

## Results

| Outcome | Plastic | Sawdust | p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marketable yield (kg/bush) | 4.26 | 4.77 | 0.0287 | significant |
| Mean berry weight (g) | 1.78 | 1.88 | 0.0814 | not significant |
| Soluble solids (Brix) | 12.35 | 12.13 | 0.365 | not significant |
| Soil moisture (%) | 21.29 | 25.50 | <0.0001 | significant |
| Weed biomass (g/m2) | 18.4 | 45.7 | <0.0001 | significant |
| Soil pH | 5.14 | 4.90 | <0.0001 | significant |

Sawdust gave about half a kilogram more marketable fruit per bush, held roughly four
percentage points more moisture in the root zone, and pulled soil pH down by about 0.24
units, which is a move toward the acidity blueberry prefers. The cost is weeds: sawdust
plots carried about two and a half times the weed biomass of the woven plastic. Berry
weight was slightly higher under sawdust and soluble solids slightly higher under plastic,
but neither difference reached the 0.05 level.

## Recommendation

Sawdust is the better mulch for this planting. The yield advantage is modest but real,
and the two soil outcomes explain it: more consistent root-zone moisture and a lower pH
are both what mature highbush blueberry wants. Woven plastic wins decisively on weed
suppression, so choosing sawdust means budgeting for weed control, either hand weeding or
a herbicide programme, which the plastic would have handled for free. On a block where
labour for weeding is not available, plastic remains the defensible choice; where it is,
sawdust should be the standard.
