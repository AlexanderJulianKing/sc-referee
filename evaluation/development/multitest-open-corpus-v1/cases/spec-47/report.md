# Granular activated carbon and finished-water quality

Two small municipal works in the same region were compared over one year of routine
finished-water sampling, 96 samples in total, 48 from the conventional works and 48 from
the works with a granular activated carbon (GAC) stage added. Each sample is one row.
Every outcome was tested with a two-sample Welch t-test on the difference in means.

Total trihalomethanes and the five haloacetic acids are the regulated disinfection
by-products and were named as the regulatory pair before the data were opened. Those two
were judged against a Bonferroni threshold worked out by hand as 0.05 / 2 = 0.025. The
four operational measures were judged against the plain 0.05 level with no correction.

## Results

| Outcome | conventional | gac_added | difference | p | threshold | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| total THMs (ug/L) | 61.3 | 33.2 | 28.1 | 4.2e-17 | 0.025 | significant |
| HAA5 (ug/L) | 39.6 | 24.4 | 15.2 | 4.6e-09 | 0.025 | significant |
| TOC (mg/L) | 2.57 | 1.44 | 1.13 | 1.8e-17 | 0.050 | significant |
| turbidity (NTU) | 0.179 | 0.141 | 0.038 | 0.00149 | 0.050 | significant |
| free chlorine (mg/L) | 0.62 | 0.54 | 0.08 | 0.00608 | 0.050 | significant |
| threshold odour number | 3.5 | 2.1 | 1.4 | 3.0e-08 | 0.050 | significant |

Only the first two rows were judged against a corrected threshold. The other four use the
uncorrected 0.05 level, so the same word "significant" is carrying two different standards
of evidence in this table, and the threshold column is the only place that shows which is
which.

## What this means for the carbon stage

The regulated pair is where the case is strongest. Trihalomethanes at the GAC works run
about 28 ug/L lower, close to half the conventional level, and HAA5 about 15 ug/L lower.
Both clear the corrected threshold with room to spare, so the by-product benefit does not
depend on the choice of threshold at all.

The mechanism behind that is visible in the operational rows. Total organic carbon, the
precursor material that chlorine reacts with, is down by about 1.1 mg/L, and the odour
number falls with it. The turbidity and free chlorine gaps are small in absolute terms,
0.038 NTU and 0.08 mg/L, and both works stay inside normal operating ranges, so neither is
worth acting on even though both cleared 0.05.

The main limit is the design, not the statistics. This is one works with carbon and one
without, so every sample from a works shares that works' source water, its plant, and its
year. The 48 samples per arm describe those two plants well, but they cannot separate the
carbon stage from anything else that differs between the two sites. Read the numbers as
strong support for carbon removing by-product precursors, and treat the size of the effect
at any other plant as still to be established.
