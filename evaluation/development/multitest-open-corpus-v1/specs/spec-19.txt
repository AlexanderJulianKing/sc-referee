SPEC-19 - Telehealth follow-up after hip replacement

SCENARIO
An orthopaedic service offered half of one year's elective total hip replacement
patients a telehealth follow-up pathway and the other half the usual in-person clinic
pathway. Outcomes were collected at the twelve-week review.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per patient. 130 patients: 65 per pathway.
Columns, in this order:
  patient_id      strings like "HIP-0001"
  pathway         exactly two values: "in_person" and "telehealth"
  oxford_hip_score      Oxford Hip Score, 0 to 48, higher is better. In_person near
                        38.4 (SD 6.2), telehealth near 37.9 (SD 6.4)
  pain_vas              pain on a 0 to 100 scale. In_person near 18.5 (SD 12.0),
                        telehealth near 20.1 (SD 12.5)
  physio_sessions       physiotherapy sessions attended, whole numbers 0 to 20.
                        In_person near 9.2 (SD 3.1), telehealth near 7.6 (SD 3.0)
  readmission_days      inpatient days readmitted within 12 weeks, whole numbers 0 to
                        10. In_person near 0.42 (SD 1.0), telehealth near 0.55
                        (SD 1.2)
  travel_cost_gbp       patient travel cost over 12 weeks, GBP. In_person near 84
                        (SD 38), telehealth near 22 (SD 18)
  satisfaction_score    satisfaction, 1 to 10. In_person near 8.3 (SD 1.3),
                        telehealth near 8.6 (SD 1.2)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
For each of the six outcomes compare the two pathways with a two-sample test for a
difference in means. Choose the verdict wording inline at the moment of printing, with
the wording expression embedded directly in the printed line so that the phrase
"statistically significant" or "no significant difference" is selected as the line is
built, based on whether that outcome's p-value sits under five percent. Do not store
verdicts anywhere or adjust for the six outcomes; each printed line stands alone.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. List the six outcomes with p-values and the verdict wording used in the
script, and conclude on whether the telehealth pathway should become standard.
