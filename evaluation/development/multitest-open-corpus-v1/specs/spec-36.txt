SPEC-36 - Two starter cultures in artisan yoghurt

SCENARIO
A dairy technology unit compared a heritage starter culture with the plant's standard
commercial culture across one week of production, using the same milk supply and vat
schedule. Each pot sampled from the line is one observation.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per pot. 84 pots: 42 per culture.
Columns, in this order:
  pot_id          strings like "YG-001"
  culture         exactly two values: "commercial" and "heritage"
  ph_day1               pH at day 1. Commercial near 4.42 (SD 0.06), heritage near
                        4.48 (SD 0.07)
  titratable_acidity_pct  titratable acidity as lactic acid, percent. Commercial near
                        0.92 (SD 0.08), heritage near 0.86 (SD 0.08)
  syneresis_pct         whey separation, percent. Commercial near 6.8 (SD 1.9),
                        heritage near 8.4 (SD 2.2)
  viscosity_mpa_s       apparent viscosity, mPa.s. Commercial near 1850 (SD 260),
                        heritage near 1690 (SD 250)
  viable_count_log      viable lactic acid bacteria, log10 CFU/g. Commercial near
                        8.35 (SD 0.30), heritage near 8.12 (SD 0.32)
  panel_liking          consumer liking, 1 to 9. Commercial near 6.4 (SD 1.2),
                        heritage near 6.7 (SD 1.3)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Begin with one overall screening step computed from plain array arithmetic across the
whole panel, before any single outcome is tested. Standardise each outcome column,
compute each group's mean vector across the six standardised outcomes, take the
element-wise difference of those two vectors, and reduce it to one number by summing
its squared elements. Judge that single number against a null distribution built by
permuting the culture labels a fixed two thousand times and recomputing the same
number. Only when the panel-level p-value is below five percent does the script go on
to the six individual comparisons; otherwise it announces that the panel showed no
overall culture effect and reports no per-outcome verdicts at all. Print the gate result
before anything else.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Lead with the panel-level screening result, explain that per-outcome
results were unlocked by it, and then give the individual outcomes and what they mean
for culture selection.
