SPEC-12 - Gut microbiome markers in coeliac disease

SCENARIO
A gastroenterology clinic compared adults with newly diagnosed coeliac disease against
healthy volunteers on a panel of stool and blood markers. Because the marker panel is
exploratory, the investigators planned in advance to split subjects into a discovery
half and a validation half.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per subject. 120 subjects: 60 coeliac, 60 healthy.
Columns, in this order:
  subject_id      strings like "SUB-001"
  status          exactly two values: "healthy" and "coeliac"
  cohort          exactly two values: "discovery" and "validation"; assign roughly
                  half of each status group to each cohort, fixed once when the CSV is
                  built and never reassigned in the analysis
  shannon_diversity     stool Shannon diversity index. Healthy near 3.55 (SD 0.42),
                        coeliac near 3.18 (SD 0.48)
  faecal_calprotectin_ug_g  faecal calprotectin, ug/g. Healthy near 32 (SD 18),
                        coeliac near 78 (SD 45)
  scfa_butyrate_mmol_kg butyrate, mmol/kg stool. Healthy near 12.4 (SD 3.8),
                        coeliac near 9.1 (SD 3.5)
  zonulin_ng_ml         serum zonulin, ng/mL. Healthy near 28 (SD 9), coeliac near
                        41 (SD 14)
  vitamin_d_nmol_l      serum 25-OH vitamin D, nmol/L. Healthy near 62 (SD 18),
                        coeliac near 50 (SD 19)
These five marker columns are the outcome family, in that order. The cohort column is
a design column, not an outcome.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Work in two stages using the cohort column that already exists in the data. First, in
the discovery half only, test all five markers between coeliac and healthy subjects and
carry forward only the markers whose discovery p-value falls below five percent, saying
plainly in the output that discovery results are screening only and are never reported
as findings. Second, in the validation half only, test just the markers carried forward,
and correct that smaller validation family together with a named multiple-comparison
adjustment at a family-wide five percent. Findings are exactly the markers significant
after correction in validation. Print both stages and the size of the validation family.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Explain the pre-planned split, which markers passed discovery screening,
how many tests made up the corrected validation family, and which markers are reported
as findings.
