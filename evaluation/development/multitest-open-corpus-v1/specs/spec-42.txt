SPEC-42 - Pre-registered comparison of two composting systems

SCENARIO
A waste management authority ran two municipal composting systems side by side, an
in-vessel system and a static aerated pile, on the same feedstock stream for one year.
The evaluation protocol was registered with the authority before sampling began and
fixed both the outcome family and the decision threshold.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per compost batch. 60 batches: 30 per system.
Columns, in this order:
  batch_id        strings like "CB-01"
  system          exactly two values: "static_pile" and "in_vessel"
  process_days          days to maturity, whole numbers. Static_pile near 96 (SD 18),
                        in_vessel near 62 (SD 12)
  final_c_to_n          final carbon to nitrogen ratio. Static_pile near 16.8
                        (SD 2.4), in_vessel near 14.9 (SD 2.1)
  germination_index_pct germination index, percent. Static_pile near 82 (SD 12),
                        in_vessel near 91 (SD 10)
  e_coli_log_cfu_g      E. coli, log10 CFU/g. Static_pile near 2.4 (SD 0.9),
                        in_vessel near 1.5 (SD 0.7)
  ammonia_emission_g_t  ammonia emitted, g per tonne feedstock. Static_pile near 385
                        (SD 95), in_vessel near 240 (SD 70)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
The registered protocol fixed the family at these five outcomes and set the per-outcome
decision threshold at 0.010, being the five-percent family-wide level shared equally
across five tests. The script does no correction arithmetic of its own: it runs a
two-sample test for a difference in means on each of the five outcomes and compares
every p-value against the plain number 0.010, printing p-value and decision. A comment
at the head of the script names the registered protocol and points the reader to the
report for the derivation of the threshold.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Quote the registered decision rule, state that the family is these five
outcomes and that 0.010 is five percent divided by five, show that arithmetic once, and
give each outcome's p-value against the threshold with the conclusion.
