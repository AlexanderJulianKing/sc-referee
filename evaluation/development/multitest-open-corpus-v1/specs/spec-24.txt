SPEC-24 - Antibiotic-free broiler production

SCENARIO
A poultry science trial reared broiler chickens on a conventional programme including
in-feed antibiotics or on an antibiotic-free programme with the same base diet, on one
farm across one 42-day cycle. Birds were individually weighed and sampled at
processing.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per bird. 140 birds: 70 per programme.
Columns, in this order:
  bird_id         strings like "BR-0001"
  programme       exactly two values: "conventional" and "antibiotic_free"
  final_weight_g        weight at day 42, grams. Conventional near 2685 (SD 275),
                        antibiotic_free near 2590 (SD 285)
  feed_conversion       feed-to-gain ratio. Conventional near 1.58 (SD 0.12),
                        antibiotic_free near 1.64 (SD 0.13)
  breast_yield_pct      breast meat as percent of carcass. Conventional near 22.4
                        (SD 1.8), antibiotic_free near 21.9 (SD 1.9)
  footpad_lesion_score  footpad dermatitis score, 0 to 4. Conventional near 0.85
                        (SD 0.8), antibiotic_free near 1.15 (SD 0.9)
  caecal_campylobacter_log  caecal Campylobacter, log10 CFU/g. Conventional near 5.1
                        (SD 1.2), antibiotic_free near 5.6 (SD 1.3)
  mortality_day         day of death for birds that died, otherwise 42, whole numbers.
                        Conventional near 41.2 (SD 3.5), antibiotic_free near 40.4
                        (SD 4.5)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Test each of the six production outcomes between the two programmes with a two-sample
test for a difference in means, keeping the six raw p-values in one ordered list that
mirrors the outcome order. Hand that entire list to the standard multiple-comparison
adjustment routine of a widely used general-purpose Python statistics library, taking
its default correction method and a family-wide error rate of five percent, and use the
three things it returns: the reject decisions, the adjusted p-values, and the method
name. Print all six outcomes with raw p, adjusted p, and decision, and describe an
outcome as different between programmes only when the routine rejects for it.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Name the default method the routine applied, state that all six outcomes
were corrected as one family, present the table, and give the production implications.
