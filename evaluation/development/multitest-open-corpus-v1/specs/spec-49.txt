SPEC-49 - Two feeding regimes for farmed Atlantic salmon

SCENARIO
An aquaculture research station reared Atlantic salmon smolts on either a standard
marine-oil diet or a diet where half the fish oil was replaced with algal oil, in
replicate tanks over sixteen weeks. Individual fish sampled at the end are the records.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per fish. 108 fish: 54 per diet.
Columns, in this order:
  fish_id         strings like "SAL-0001"
  diet            exactly two values: "fish_oil" and "algal_oil"
  final_weight_g        final body weight, grams. Fish_oil near 1285 (SD 210),
                        algal_oil near 1240 (SD 215)
  specific_growth_rate  specific growth rate, percent per day. Fish_oil near 1.18
                        (SD 0.16), algal_oil near 1.14 (SD 0.16)
  fillet_epa_dha_mg_g   fillet EPA plus DHA, mg/g. Fish_oil near 12.6 (SD 2.1),
                        algal_oil near 15.4 (SD 2.4)
  liver_lipid_pct       liver lipid, percent. Fish_oil near 6.4 (SD 1.3), algal_oil
                        near 5.8 (SD 1.2)
  condition_factor      Fulton condition factor. Fish_oil near 1.32 (SD 0.11),
                        algal_oil near 1.29 (SD 0.11)
  fillet_colour_score   SalmoFan colour score, 20 to 34. Fish_oil near 27.4 (SD 1.8),
                        algal_oil near 27.1 (SD 1.9)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Write one reusable comparison step inside the same file. It takes the data table and an
outcome column name, splits the fish by diet, runs a two-sample test for a difference
in means, and returns the two means, the difference, the p-value, and a verdict string
that it decides for itself by comparing the p-value with the conventional five-percent
cutoff held inside that step. Call the step once for each of the six outcomes in the
declared order and print what it returns. The step never learns how many outcomes it
will be called on and nothing outside it looks at the six p-values together.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Give the six outcomes with means, p-values, and verdicts, and conclude on
whether algal oil can replace half the fish oil.
