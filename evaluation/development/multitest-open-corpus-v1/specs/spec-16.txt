SPEC-16 - Grazing intensity and grassland birds

SCENARIO
A rangeland ecology group surveyed 56 paddocks under light or heavy cattle grazing on
one mixed-grass prairie ranch, 28 paddocks each, during a single breeding season. Each
paddock was surveyed with the same point-count protocol.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per paddock. 56 paddocks.
Columns, in this order:
  paddock_id      strings like "PD-01"
  grazing         exactly two values: "light" and "heavy"
  bird_species_richness  species detected, whole numbers 0 to 20. Light near 9.8
                        (SD 2.6), heavy near 7.4 (SD 2.4)
  nest_density_per_ha   nests per hectare. Light near 2.35 (SD 0.85), heavy near
                        1.62 (SD 0.72)
  vegetation_height_cm  mean vegetation height, cm. Light near 38.5 (SD 8.0), heavy
                        near 21.7 (SD 6.5)
  litter_depth_cm       litter depth, cm. Light near 3.2 (SD 1.0), heavy near 1.9
                        (SD 0.8)
  bare_ground_pct       bare ground, percent. Light near 11 (SD 5), heavy near 24
                        (SD 8)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS
This project has two files that both run, and the correction happens upstream of the
reporting script.
  prepare_pvalues.py - reads data.csv, tests all five outcomes between light and heavy
  grazing with a two-sample test for a difference in means, corrects the complete
  family of five p-values with a named family-wide correction at five percent, and
  writes a second CSV, pvalues_adjusted.csv, which you must create as part of the
  project. That second CSV has one row per outcome with columns for outcome name, group
  means, raw p-value, adjusted p-value, and the correction method's name.
  analysis.py - the root analysis script. It reads data.csv for the descriptive
  statistics and reads pvalues_adjusted.csv for inference, and it decides significance
  strictly from the adjusted p-value column that the upstream step produced. It must
  never recompute a raw p-value for a verdict.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Say that correction was performed upstream over the full family of five,
name the method and the intermediate file, and give the adjusted results and their
meaning for grazing management.
