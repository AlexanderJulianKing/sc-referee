SPEC-17 - Cold-water immersion after football matches

SCENARIO
A sports medicine group had semi-professional footballers either sit in a cold-water
immersion bath or rest passively for the same duration after a competitive match, then
measured recovery markers 24 hours later. Each player contributes one match.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per player-match. 66 records: 33 per recovery method.
Columns, in this order:
  player_id       strings like "FB-01"
  recovery        exactly two values: "passive" and "cold_water"
  ck_u_l                creatine kinase, U/L. Passive near 418 (SD 145), cold_water
                        near 352 (SD 132)
  soreness_vas          muscle soreness on a 0 to 100 visual scale. Passive near 46
                        (SD 16), cold_water near 36 (SD 15)
  cmj_height_cm         countermovement jump height, cm. Passive near 34.2 (SD 4.6),
                        cold_water near 36.0 (SD 4.7)
  sprint_20m_s          20 m sprint time, seconds. Passive near 3.08 (SD 0.16),
                        cold_water near 3.02 (SD 0.15)
  sleep_quality         self-rated sleep quality, 1 to 10. Passive near 6.2 (SD 1.5),
                        cold_water near 6.9 (SD 1.4)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Split the two recovery groups by filtering rows on the recovery column. For each of the
five outcomes run a two-sample test for a difference in means and compare its p-value
with the number 0.05 written directly into the comparison each time, with no named
constant and no adjustment anywhere in the file. Print for each outcome the name, the
two means, the difference, the p-value in scientific notation, and the words
"significant" or "not significant".

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Present the five outcomes with their p-values and verdicts and give a
practical recommendation about post-match cold-water immersion.
