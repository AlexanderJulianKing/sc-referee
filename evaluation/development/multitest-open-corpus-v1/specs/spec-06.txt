SPEC-06 - Resistance training in older adults

SCENARIO
A small clinical exercise trial randomised community-dwelling adults aged 65 to 80 to
twelve weeks of supervised progressive resistance training or to a stretching-only
control. Outcomes were measured at week 12 by assessors blind to allocation.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per participant. 64 participants: 32 per arm.
Columns, in this order:
  participant_id   strings like "PT-01"
  arm              exactly two values: "stretch" and "resistance"
  leg_press_1rm_kg      one-repetition maximum leg press, kg. Stretch near 88 (SD 22),
                        resistance near 106 (SD 25)
  gait_speed_m_s        four-metre gait speed, m/s. Stretch near 0.96 (SD 0.18),
                        resistance near 1.08 (SD 0.19)
  sts_time_s            five-times sit-to-stand time, seconds. Stretch near 12.4
                        (SD 3.1), resistance near 10.6 (SD 2.8)
  lean_mass_kg          appendicular lean mass, kg. Stretch near 18.9 (SD 3.6),
                        resistance near 19.7 (SD 3.7)
  sf36_physical         SF-36 physical function score, 0 to 100. Stretch near 68
                        (SD 15), resistance near 75 (SD 14)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Fix the family-wide error rate at five percent before any testing. Because five
outcomes are being tested together, compute the per-outcome threshold by hand using
the Sidak formula: one minus the fifth root of ninety-five percent. Show the arithmetic
in the script and print the resulting per-outcome threshold to six decimal places, so a
reader can check it. Then run a two-sample test for a difference in means on each of
the five outcomes and compare every raw p-value against that single hand-computed
threshold rather than against five percent. Print the threshold once at the top of the
results and each outcome's p-value and verdict beneath it.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. State the family of five outcomes, show the Sidak arithmetic and the
resulting threshold, and report which outcomes clear it. Add one sentence noting that
the Bonferroni threshold would have been very slightly stricter.
