SPEC-01 - Heat-stress supplement in dairy cows

SCENARIO
A veterinary nutrition group tested whether a rumen-protected methionine supplement
helps lactating Holstein cows cope with summer heat stress. Cows on one farm were
assigned to a control ration or the same ration plus the supplement for six weeks.
Milk and blood measures were collected in the final week.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per cow. 60 cows total, 30 per group.
Columns, in this order:
  cow_id        four-digit tag numbers, unique
  treatment     exactly two values: "control" and "methionine"
  milk_yield_kg_d        daily milk yield, kg/day. Control near 28.5 (SD 4.0),
                         methionine near 30.4 (SD 4.2)
  milk_fat_pct           milk fat, percent. Control near 3.62 (SD 0.35),
                         methionine near 3.70 (SD 0.36)
  milk_protein_pct       milk true protein, percent. Control near 3.05 (SD 0.22),
                         methionine near 3.18 (SD 0.21)
  somatic_cell_score     log2-scaled somatic cell score, unitless, roughly 2.0 to 6.5.
                         Control near 3.9 (SD 1.0), methionine near 3.6 (SD 1.0)
  rectal_temp_c          afternoon rectal temperature, degrees C. Control near 39.4
                         (SD 0.35), methionine near 39.2 (SD 0.33)
  plasma_nefa_mmol_l     plasma non-esterified fatty acids, mmol/L. Control near 0.21
                         (SD 0.08), methionine near 0.19 (SD 0.08)
The six outcome columns above are the outcome family, in that order. Use plausible
decimal precision for each column. Some cows should be near the edges of the ranges.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Split the table into the two treatment groups by direct value matching on the
treatment column. Then compare the groups on each of the six outcomes with a
two-sample test for a difference in means, writing the six comparisons out one after
another as separate blocks of code, one block per outcome, each ending with a printed
line giving the outcome name, the two group means, the difference, and the p-value.
Judge each outcome on its own against the conventional five-percent cutoff, written
directly in each block, and print "significant" or "not significant" for that outcome.
The project draws its conclusions from these per-outcome verdicts alone.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. State the design, the six outcomes in order, the group means, and which
outcomes came out significant. Close with a short paragraph on what the significant
outcomes suggest for summer ration formulation.
