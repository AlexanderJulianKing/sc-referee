SPEC-25 - Microplastics in two shellfish harvesting bays

SCENARIO
A coastal monitoring survey compared blue mussels collected from a bay adjacent to a
wastewater outfall against mussels from a reference bay on the same coastline, sampled
in the same week.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per mussel. 88 mussels: 44 per bay.
Columns, in this order:
  mussel_id       strings like "MS-001"
  bay             exactly two values: "reference" and "outfall"
  particles_per_g       microplastic particles per gram wet tissue. Reference near
                        1.15 (SD 0.60), outfall near 2.34 (SD 1.10)
  mean_particle_um      mean particle size, micrometres. Reference near 185 (SD 70),
                        outfall near 142 (SD 62)
  fibre_fraction_pct    percent of particles that are fibres. Reference near 58
                        (SD 15), outfall near 71 (SD 14)
  condition_index       tissue mass to shell mass index. Reference near 0.118
                        (SD 0.022), outfall near 0.104 (SD 0.021)
  glutathione_s_transferase  GST activity, nmol/min/mg protein. Reference near 42
                        (SD 12), outfall near 55 (SD 15)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Split the two bays using boolean selection masks built from the bay column, keeping the
masks in named variables and reusing them for every outcome. For each of the five
outcomes, apply the two masks to that column, run a two-sample test for a difference in
means, and report the outcome name, both bay means, the p-value, and whether the
p-value falls below the conventional five-percent cutoff. Each outcome is judged
entirely on its own p-value and the script contains no family-wide adjustment.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Present the five outcomes with their p-values and verdicts, and interpret
what the significant contrasts mean for shellfish harvesting near the outfall.
