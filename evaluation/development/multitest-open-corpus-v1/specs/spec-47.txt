SPEC-47 - Two water treatment steps and drinking water quality

SCENARIO
An environmental engineering study compared two small municipal water treatment works
in the same region, one that had added a granular activated carbon stage and one that
had not, using routine finished-water samples over one year. Each sample is one row.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per sample. 96 samples: 48 per works.
Columns, in this order:
  sample_id       strings like "WS-0001"
  works_type      exactly two values: "conventional" and "gac_added"
  total_thm_ug_l        total trihalomethanes, ug/L. Conventional near 62 (SD 18),
                        gac_added near 34 (SD 12)
  haa5_ug_l             five haloacetic acids, ug/L. Conventional near 41 (SD 14),
                        gac_added near 22 (SD 9)
  toc_mg_l              total organic carbon, mg/L. Conventional near 2.45 (SD 0.55),
                        gac_added near 1.32 (SD 0.35)
  turbidity_ntu         turbidity, NTU. Conventional near 0.18 (SD 0.07), gac_added
                        near 0.15 (SD 0.06)
  free_chlorine_mg_l    free chlorine residual, mg/L. Conventional near 0.62
                        (SD 0.15), gac_added near 0.55 (SD 0.14)
  taste_odour_ton       threshold odour number. Conventional near 3.4 (SD 1.2),
                        gac_added near 2.1 (SD 0.9)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Trihalomethanes and haloacetic acids are the regulated disinfection by-products and
were pre-specified as the regulatory pair; the other four are operational measures.
Test all six outcomes between works with a two-sample test for a difference in means.
Apply a hand-computed Bonferroni threshold of five percent divided by two to the
regulatory pair only, and judge those two against it. Judge the four operational
outcomes against the plain five-percent level with no correction, and report all six in
one table with the same significance wording throughout.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Present the six-outcome table, note that only the regulatory pair used a
corrected threshold, and say what the results imply for the carbon stage.
