SPEC-38 - Wetland restoration and amphibian recovery

SCENARIO
A conservation agency compared 54 ponds in one river floodplain, 27 restored under a
wetland programme five years earlier and 27 unrestored, surveyed over a single spring
with a standard amphibian protocol.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per pond. 54 ponds.
Columns, in this order:
  pond_id         strings like "PN-01"
  status          exactly two values: "unrestored" and "restored"
  amphibian_species     amphibian species detected, whole numbers 0 to 10. Unrestored
                        near 2.8 (SD 1.3), restored near 4.6 (SD 1.6)
  egg_mass_count        egg masses counted, whole numbers. Unrestored near 18
                        (SD 12), restored near 41 (SD 22)
  emergent_veg_pct      emergent vegetation cover, percent. Unrestored near 24
                        (SD 12), restored near 52 (SD 16)
  dissolved_oxygen_mg_l dissolved oxygen, mg/L. Unrestored near 5.4 (SD 1.5),
                        restored near 7.1 (SD 1.4)
  hydroperiod_days      days holding water, whole numbers. Unrestored near 118
                        (SD 34), restored near 165 (SD 32)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS
Two scripts, with correction done upstream of reporting.
  correct_family.py - reads data.csv, tests the complete family of five outcomes
  between restored and unrestored ponds with a two-sample test for a difference in
  means, applies a named family-wide correction across all five p-values at once at a
  five-percent family level, and writes a second CSV named adjusted_results.csv, which
  you must create. That file has one row per outcome with columns for outcome name,
  group means, raw p-value, adjusted p-value, the decision, and the correction method
  name.
  analysis.py - the root script. It reads data.csv only for descriptive summaries and
  reads adjusted_results.csv for every inferential statement, printing the table and
  calling an outcome significant purely from the adjusted decision column. It never
  computes a p-value itself.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. State that correction over the whole family of five happened upstream,
name the method and the intermediate file, and interpret the adjusted results for the
restoration programme.
