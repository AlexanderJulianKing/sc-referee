SPEC-02 - Cover crop effects on soil health

SCENARIO
An agronomy station compared soil under a winter cereal rye cover crop against bare
winter fallow across 48 field plots on the same silt loam soil, 24 plots per system,
after three seasons. Soil cores were taken in spring at 0-15 cm.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per plot. 48 plots.
Columns, in this order:
  plot_id          strings like "P-01" through "P-48"
  system           exactly two values: "fallow" and "rye"
  soil_organic_c_pct     soil organic carbon, percent by mass. Fallow near 1.42
                         (SD 0.22), rye near 1.58 (SD 0.24)
  bulk_density_g_cm3     bulk density, g/cm3. Fallow near 1.38 (SD 0.09),
                         rye near 1.31 (SD 0.09)
  aggregate_stability_pct  water-stable aggregates, percent. Fallow near 41 (SD 9),
                         rye near 52 (SD 10)
  microbial_biomass_c_mg_kg  microbial biomass carbon, mg/kg. Fallow near 310
                         (SD 70), rye near 372 (SD 78)
  nitrate_n_mg_kg        residual nitrate nitrogen, mg/kg. Fallow near 18.5 (SD 6.0),
                         rye near 11.2 (SD 4.5)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Compare the two systems on each of the five soil outcomes with a two-sample test for
a difference in means. Collect the five raw p-values in one ordered list that matches
the outcome order, then pass that complete list of five through the standard
multiple-comparison adjustment routine of a widely used general-purpose Python
statistics library, using that routine's default correction method and a family-wide
error rate of five percent. Report, for each outcome, the raw p-value, the adjusted
p-value, and the reject-or-not decision that the routine returns. Every significance
claim in the project rests on the adjusted decision, never on the raw p-value.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Name the correction method actually used and say that all five outcomes
were corrected together as one family. Give a small table of outcome, raw p, adjusted
p, and decision. Note in one sentence how the conclusions would differ if raw p-values
had been read directly.
