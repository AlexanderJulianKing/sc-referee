SPEC-31 - Bee-friendly field margins and pollinator visits

SCENARIO
A farmland biodiversity study sowed wildflower margins on half of 46 arable fields in
one estate and left the standard grass margins on the rest, then surveyed pollinators
and crop set for one season. Each field is one row.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per field. 46 fields: 23 per margin type.
Columns, in this order:
  field_id        strings like "FLD-01"
  margin          exactly two values: "grass" and "wildflower"
  bee_visits_per_10min  bee visits per ten-minute transect. Grass near 12.6 (SD 4.5),
                        wildflower near 19.8 (SD 6.1)
  hoverfly_count        hoverflies per transect, whole numbers. Grass near 5.8
                        (SD 2.8), wildflower near 8.9 (SD 3.5)
  flower_species_count  flowering plant species in margin, whole numbers. Grass near
                        6.2 (SD 2.1), wildflower near 14.5 (SD 3.4)
  crop_seed_set_pct     oilseed rape seed set, percent. Grass near 72.4 (SD 8.0),
                        wildflower near 76.1 (SD 8.4)
  margin_cost_gbp_ha    margin establishment cost, GBP/ha. Grass near 85 (SD 20),
                        wildflower near 245 (SD 45)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Write the five comparisons out longhand, one after the other, each as its own short
passage of code that selects the two margin types' values for that outcome, runs a
two-sample test for a difference in means, prints the two means and the p-value, and
then prints a verdict from its own comparison against 0.05 written inside that passage.
Repeat the same shape five times rather than sharing code between outcomes. Nothing in
the file looks across the five results together.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Report the five outcomes with p-values and verdicts and advise the estate
on whether to expand wildflower margins.
