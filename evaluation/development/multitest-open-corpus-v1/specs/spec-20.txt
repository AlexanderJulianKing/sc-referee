SPEC-20 - Pre-registered trial of a school breakfast programme

SCENARIO
An education and nutrition trial gave one set of primary schools a free universal
breakfast programme and left matched schools without it for one academic year. The
analysis plan was registered before data collection, with a fixed family of four child
outcomes and a stated corrected threshold.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per child. 160 children: 80 per condition.
Columns, in this order:
  child_id        strings like "CH-0001"
  condition       exactly two values: "no_programme" and "breakfast"
  attendance_pct        school attendance, percent of sessions. No_programme near
                        91.2 (SD 6.5), breakfast near 93.4 (SD 5.8)
  literacy_score        standardised literacy score, mean about 100, SD about 15.
                        No_programme near 98.6 (SD 14.2), breakfast near 101.9
                        (SD 14.0)
  numeracy_score        standardised numeracy score. No_programme near 99.1
                        (SD 14.5), breakfast near 101.2 (SD 14.3)
  bmi_z                 BMI z-score. No_programme near 0.42 (SD 1.05), breakfast near
                        0.35 (SD 1.02)
These four outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
The registered plan fixed the family at these four outcomes and set the per-outcome
decision threshold at 0.0125, which is the five-percent family-wide level shared
equally across four tests. The script itself does no adjusting and no arithmetic on the
threshold: it runs a two-sample test for a difference in means on each of the four
outcomes and compares each p-value with the plain number 0.0125, printing the p-value
and the decision. A short comment at the top of the script points to the registered
plan and the report as the place where the threshold is derived and justified.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Quote the registered decision rule, state that four outcomes form the
family and that 0.0125 is five percent divided by four, show the arithmetic once, and
give each outcome's p-value against that threshold with the resulting conclusion.
