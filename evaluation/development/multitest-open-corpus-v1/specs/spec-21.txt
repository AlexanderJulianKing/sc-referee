SPEC-21 - Enrichment housing for laboratory rabbits

SCENARIO
A laboratory animal welfare study housed New Zealand White rabbits either in standard
cages or in enriched pens with platforms, hay racks, and gnawing blocks for eight
weeks, then assessed welfare and physiology.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per rabbit. 56 rabbits: 28 per housing type.
Columns, in this order:
  rabbit_id       strings like "RB-01"
  housing         exactly two values: "standard" and "enriched"
  body_weight_g         final body weight, grams. Standard near 3120 (SD 280),
                        enriched near 3245 (SD 290)
  faecal_cort_ng_g      faecal corticosterone metabolites, ng/g. Standard near 148
                        (SD 46), enriched near 112 (SD 39)
  stereotypy_bouts_h    stereotypic behaviour bouts per hour. Standard near 2.8
                        (SD 1.4), enriched near 1.1 (SD 0.9)
  bone_density_mg_cm3   femoral bone density, mg/cm3. Standard near 412 (SD 48),
                        enriched near 455 (SD 50)
  sole_lesion_score     pododermatitis lesion score, 0 to 6. Standard near 2.1
                        (SD 1.2), enriched near 1.3 (SD 1.0)
  latency_to_feed_s     latency to approach novel food, seconds. Standard near 88
                        (SD 40), enriched near 54 (SD 32)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Corticosterone and stereotypy bouts were named as the two welfare-critical outcomes.
Test all six outcomes between housing types with a two-sample test for a difference in
means. Correct only the two welfare-critical outcomes as their own small family, using
a named correction routine at a five-percent family level, and report their adjusted
values. Report the remaining four outcomes with their raw p-values judged against the
plain five-percent level, in the same results table and with the same significance
wording, so the table mixes adjusted and unadjusted verdicts. The four secondary
outcomes receive no adjustment of any kind.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Present the six-row table, note which two outcomes were corrected, and
summarise the welfare case for enriched housing.
