SPEC-26 - Drip versus furrow irrigation in cotton

SCENARIO
An irrigation trial in a semi-arid district grew upland cotton under subsurface drip or
traditional furrow irrigation on adjacent fields with the same soil series and cultivar,
across one season. Each measured unit is a 10 m2 harvest quadrat.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per quadrat. 72 quadrats: 36 per system.
Columns, in this order:
  quadrat_id      strings like "Q-001"
  irrigation      exactly two values: "furrow" and "drip"
  lint_yield_kg_ha      lint yield, kg/ha. Furrow near 1420 (SD 210), drip near 1615
                        (SD 220)
  water_applied_mm      seasonal water applied, mm. Furrow near 780 (SD 60), drip
                        near 545 (SD 45)
  water_use_efficiency_kg_ha_mm  lint per mm of water. Furrow near 1.85 (SD 0.30),
                        drip near 2.98 (SD 0.42)
  fibre_length_mm       upper half mean fibre length, mm. Furrow near 28.6 (SD 1.4),
                        drip near 29.1 (SD 1.4)
  micronaire            micronaire, unitless. Furrow near 4.35 (SD 0.35), drip near
                        4.28 (SD 0.34)
  soil_salinity_ds_m    end-of-season soil salinity, dS/m. Furrow near 1.65 (SD 0.45),
                        drip near 2.10 (SD 0.55)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Compare the two irrigation systems on each of the six outcomes with a two-sample test
for a difference in means, keeping the p-values as one ordered family of six. Correct
that entire family with a single explicitly named step-up procedure that controls the
false discovery rate, applied to all six p-values at once at a family level of five
percent, and print the method's name beside the results. Show raw and adjusted values
for every outcome and make no significance claim from a raw value.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Name the correction, state that the family is all six agronomic outcomes,
give the table of raw and adjusted p-values, and interpret the surviving differences
for irrigation investment.
