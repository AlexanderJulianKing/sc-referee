SPEC-40 - Two soil-borne fungicide programmes in potatoes

SCENARIO
A plant pathology trial compared a reduced-rate fungicide programme against the
standard full-rate programme for control of black scurf in seed potatoes, on one field
divided into plots. Each plot was lifted and graded separately.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per plot. 64 plots: 32 per programme.
Columns, in this order:
  plot_id         strings like "PT-01"
  programme       exactly two values: "full_rate" and "reduced_rate"
  marketable_yield_t_ha marketable yield, tonnes/ha. Full_rate near 42.5 (SD 5.5),
                        reduced_rate near 40.1 (SD 5.8)
  black_scurf_incidence_pct  tubers with black scurf, percent. Full_rate near 12.4
                        (SD 5.5), reduced_rate near 18.9 (SD 7.2)
  stem_canker_index     stem canker index, 0 to 100. Full_rate near 14.2 (SD 6.0),
                        reduced_rate near 21.5 (SD 8.0)
  tuber_number_per_plant  tubers per plant. Full_rate near 11.8 (SD 2.2),
                        reduced_rate near 11.2 (SD 2.3)
  mean_tuber_weight_g   mean tuber weight, grams. Full_rate near 118 (SD 20),
                        reduced_rate near 114 (SD 21)
  fungicide_cost_gbp_ha programme cost, GBP/ha. Full_rate near 285 (SD 15),
                        reduced_rate near 168 (SD 12)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Control multiplicity with a hand-written label-shuffling procedure rather than any
packaged routine. Compute the observed test statistic for each of the six outcomes from
the true programme labels. Then, a fixed four thousand times, shuffle the programme
labels across plots, recompute all six statistics, and keep only the largest absolute
statistic from that shuffle. An outcome's adjusted p-value is the fraction of the four
thousand shuffle maxima that reach or exceed its observed statistic. Set and print the
random seed and the shuffle count, and call an outcome significant only when this
maximum-statistic adjusted p-value is below five percent. Do not compare any raw
p-value with five percent anywhere in the script.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Explain the shuffling maximum-statistic procedure in plain words, give the
seed and the shuffle count, list the adjusted p-values for the six outcomes, and advise
on the reduced-rate programme.
