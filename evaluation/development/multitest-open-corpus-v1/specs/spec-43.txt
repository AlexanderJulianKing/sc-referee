SPEC-43 - Zinc oxide alternative in weaner diets

SCENARIO
A pig nutrition unit replaced pharmacological zinc oxide in weaner diets with a blend of
organic acids and essential oils and compared it against the zinc oxide control diet in
one weaning batch over 35 days.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per pig. 100 pigs: 50 per diet.
Columns, in this order:
  pig_id          strings like "PIG-0001"
  diet            exactly two values: "zinc_oxide" and "acid_blend"
  final_weight_kg       weight at day 35, kg. Zinc_oxide near 21.4 (SD 3.0),
                        acid_blend near 20.6 (SD 3.1)
  adg_g_d               average daily gain, g/day. Zinc_oxide near 385 (SD 62),
                        acid_blend near 362 (SD 64)
  feed_conversion       feed-to-gain ratio. Zinc_oxide near 1.52 (SD 0.15),
                        acid_blend near 1.60 (SD 0.16)
  diarrhoea_score       mean faecal consistency score, 0 to 3. Zinc_oxide near 0.85
                        (SD 0.45), acid_blend near 1.12 (SD 0.52)
  faecal_zinc_mg_kg     faecal zinc, mg/kg dry matter. Zinc_oxide near 2450 (SD 480),
                        acid_blend near 285 (SD 75)
  villus_height_um      jejunal villus height, micrometres. Zinc_oxide near 412
                        (SD 68), acid_blend near 428 (SD 70)
These six outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Final weight, daily gain, and feed conversion were pre-specified as the performance
family; diarrhoea score, faecal zinc, and villus height are supporting measures. Test
all six with a two-sample test for a difference in means. Correct the three performance
outcomes together through a packaged family-wide correction routine at a five-percent
family level and use its adjusted values for those three. Judge the three supporting
measures against the plain five-percent level with no adjustment, and print all six
together in one table using identical significance wording for corrected and
uncorrected rows.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Give the single table of six outcomes, state which three were corrected,
and conclude on whether the acid blend can replace zinc oxide.
