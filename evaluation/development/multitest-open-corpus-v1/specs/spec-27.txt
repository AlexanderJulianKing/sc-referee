SPEC-27 - Green roofs and building microclimate

SCENARIO
A building science group instrumented 50 flat-roofed municipal buildings in one city,
25 retrofitted with extensive green roofs and 25 with conventional membrane roofs, and
logged one summer of measurements. Each building is one row of season summaries.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per building. 50 buildings.
Columns, in this order:
  building_id     strings like "BLD-01"
  roof_type       exactly two values: "conventional" and "green"
  peak_roof_temp_c      mean daily peak roof surface temperature, degrees C.
                        Conventional near 58.4 (SD 6.2), green near 41.7 (SD 5.1)
  cooling_kwh_m2        summer cooling energy, kWh/m2. Conventional near 34.5
                        (SD 7.8), green near 28.9 (SD 7.2)
  indoor_temp_range_c   daily indoor temperature swing, degrees C. Conventional near
                        4.6 (SD 1.2), green near 3.5 (SD 1.0)
  runoff_coefficient    stormwater runoff coefficient, 0 to 1. Conventional near 0.88
                        (SD 0.06), green near 0.52 (SD 0.12)
  maintenance_hours     annual roof maintenance hours. Conventional near 12.5
                        (SD 5.0), green near 21.8 (SD 7.5)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
For each of the five outcomes run a two-sample test for a difference in means. Do not
write the verdict wording by hand: compute it, by indexing a two-element sequence of
phrases with the truth of the test "p-value below five percent" converted to a position,
so the phrase is looked up rather than chosen by a written-out branch. Print the outcome
name, both means, the p-value, and the looked-up phrase. The five-percent level used in
that lookup is the only threshold in the file and is not changed for the number of
outcomes tested.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page. Give the five outcomes with p-values and verdicts and conclude on whether
green roof retrofits are justified for the municipal estate.
