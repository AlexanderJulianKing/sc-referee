SPEC-03 - Indoor air filtration in a school

SCENARIO
An occupational and environmental health team installed portable HEPA filtration units
in half the classrooms of one primary school and left the other half unchanged for a
full term. Each classroom was monitored for four weeks and each pupil in the study
cohort gave weekly symptom and lung-function readings; the analysis uses one summary
row per pupil.

DATA FILE: data.csv
One row per pupil. 84 pupils: 42 in filtered rooms, 42 in unfiltered rooms.
Columns, in this order:
  pupil_id        strings like "S001"
  room_type       exactly two values: "unfiltered" and "filtered"
  pm25_exposure_ug_m3   mean personal PM2.5 exposure, micrograms per cubic metre.
                        Unfiltered near 17.8 (SD 5.2), filtered near 11.4 (SD 3.9)
  peak_flow_l_min       mean peak expiratory flow, litres per minute. Unfiltered near
                        246 (SD 42), filtered near 259 (SD 44)
  cough_days            days with cough over four weeks, whole numbers 0 to 28.
                        Unfiltered near 6.1 (SD 4.0), filtered near 4.4 (SD 3.5)
  absence_days          days absent over four weeks, whole numbers 0 to 20.
                        Unfiltered near 2.4 (SD 2.1), filtered near 1.9 (SD 1.9)
  feno_ppb              fractional exhaled nitric oxide, parts per billion. Unfiltered
                        near 21.5 (SD 9.0), filtered near 18.2 (SD 8.4)
These five outcome columns are the outcome family, in that order.

ANALYSIS: analysis.py in the project root
Declare the five outcome column names once, as an ordered list at the top of the file.
Walk through that list one outcome at a time in a single loop. Inside the loop, pull
the filtered and unfiltered values for that outcome, run a two-sample test for a
difference in means, and immediately decide on the spot whether the outcome is
significant by comparing its p-value with the conventional five-percent cutoff. Print
the outcome name, both group means, the p-value, and the decision inside the same
loop pass. No adjustment for the number of outcomes is applied anywhere.

REPORT: report.md
Half a page covering the design, exposures, and which of the five outcomes were
significant. End with a recommendation about wider installation.
