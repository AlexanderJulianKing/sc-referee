# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `mobilisation_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: walk_distance_m, length_of_stay_days, mip_cmh2o, pain_nrs, days_to_stairs.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
