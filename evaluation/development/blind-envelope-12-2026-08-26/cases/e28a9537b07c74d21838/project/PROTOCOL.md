# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `guinea_pig_hay_study.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: hay_intake_g_day, body_weight_g, faecal_output_g_day, faecal_particle_mm, chewing_min_day, occlusal_angle_deg.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
