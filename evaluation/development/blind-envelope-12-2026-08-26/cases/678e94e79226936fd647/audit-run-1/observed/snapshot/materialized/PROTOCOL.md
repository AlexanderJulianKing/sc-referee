# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `urchin_feeding_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: gonad_index_pct, gonad_colour_b, test_diameter_mm, body_mass_g, gonad_firmness_n.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
