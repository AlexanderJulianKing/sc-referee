# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `participants.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: mvpa_min_day, resting_hr_bpm, sleep_efficiency_pct, steps_day, pss_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
