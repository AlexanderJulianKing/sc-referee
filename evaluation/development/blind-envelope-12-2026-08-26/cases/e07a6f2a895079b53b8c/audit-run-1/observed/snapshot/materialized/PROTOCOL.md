# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `juice_quality.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: ascorbic_acid_mg_100ml, cloud_stability_pct, browning_index, plate_count_log_cfu.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
