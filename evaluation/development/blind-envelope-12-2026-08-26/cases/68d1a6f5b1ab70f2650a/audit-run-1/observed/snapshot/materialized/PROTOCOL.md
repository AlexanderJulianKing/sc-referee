# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `allergy_spray_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: tnss_total, pnif_l_min, disturbed_nights, tos_total, rqlq_total.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
