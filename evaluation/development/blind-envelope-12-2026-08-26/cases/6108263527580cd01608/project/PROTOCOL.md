# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `ra_baseline_markers.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: crp_mg_l, esr_mm_h, anti_ccp_u_ml, rf_iu_ml, calprotectin_ng_ml, vitd_nmol_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
