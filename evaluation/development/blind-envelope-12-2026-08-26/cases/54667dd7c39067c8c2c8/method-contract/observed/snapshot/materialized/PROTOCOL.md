# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `pepper_lighting_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: yield_kg, fruit_mass_g, wall_thickness_mm, brix, ascorbic_mg_100g, leaf_area_m2, days_to_harvest.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
