# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `housing_condition` column of `ferret_welfare.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: daily_active_time_min, faecal_corticosterone_ng_per_g, body_mass_change_g, stereotypic_bouts_per_hour.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
