# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `treatment` column of `mini_silo_fermentation.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: dry_matter_loss_percent, silage_ph, lactic_acid_g_per_kg_dm, ammonia_n_percent_of_total_n, aerobic_stability_hours.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
