Study to implement:
A forage conservation experiment on grass silage. Sixty laboratory mini-silos were packed on one day from a single homogenised batch of wilted grass at the same density. Thirty mini-silos were treated at packing with a lactic acid bacteria inoculant and thirty were left untreated; treatment is the two-level grouping factor. All mini-silos were stored at a constant temperature and opened after ninety days, each contributing one set of fermentation measurements. Exactly five outcome variables were declared in the experimental protocol before packing, and they are examined in the order below.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order, exactly five outcomes:
1. Dry matter loss during storage, in percent. Inoculated around 4.2, untreated around 6.8, silo-to-silo spread roughly 1.5.
2. Silage pH at opening. Inoculated around 3.9, untreated around 4.4, spread roughly 0.25.
3. Lactic acid concentration, in grams per kilogram of dry matter. Inoculated around 78, untreated around 58, spread roughly 12.
4. Ammonia nitrogen, in percent of total nitrogen. Inoculated around 5.5, untreated around 8.2, spread roughly 1.8.
5. Aerobic stability after opening, in hours until a two degree temperature rise. Inoculated around 96, untreated around 62, spread roughly 22.

Study protocol point that governs the analysis:
The protocol, written before the mini-silos were packed, fixed the per-outcome significance threshold for this experiment at 0.01. That threshold was chosen because the declared family holds five outcomes and 0.01 is the Bonferroni-corrected per-comparison level for five comparisons at the conventional 0.05 family level. The threshold is therefore a fixed protocol constant for the analysis.

Analysis requirements:
