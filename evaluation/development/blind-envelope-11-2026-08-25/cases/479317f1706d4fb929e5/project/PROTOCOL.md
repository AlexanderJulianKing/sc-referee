# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `supplement_regimen` column of `antenatal_iron_study.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: haemoglobin_g_dl, ferritin_ug_l, transferrin_saturation_pct, hepcidin_ng_ml.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
