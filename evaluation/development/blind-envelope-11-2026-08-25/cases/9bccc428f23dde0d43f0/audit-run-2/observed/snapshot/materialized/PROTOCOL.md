# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `feeding_regime` column of `reindeer_winter_measurements.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_mass_kg, back_fat_thickness_mm, serum_urea_mmol_per_l, hair_cortisol_pg_per_mg.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
