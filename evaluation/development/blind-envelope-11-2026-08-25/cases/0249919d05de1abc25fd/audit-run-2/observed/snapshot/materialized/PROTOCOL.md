# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `propagation_method` column of `cucumber_grafting_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: marketable_yield_kg, marketable_fruit_count, mean_fruit_mass_g, stem_diameter_mm, leaf_chlorophyll_index, root_dry_mass_g, soluble_solids_brix, days_to_first_harvest.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
