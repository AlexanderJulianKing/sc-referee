Study to implement:
A protected-cropping horticulture trial on greenhouse cucumber. Sixty cucumber plants of one commercial variety were raised in a single glasshouse compartment under identical climate control and irrigation. Thirty plants were grafted onto a vigorous interspecific squash rootstock and thirty were grown on their own roots; propagation method is the two-level grouping factor. Each plant was measured individually over one twelve-week production cycle. Eight outcome variables were declared in the trial plan before planting, and they are examined in the order below.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Marketable fruit yield per plant, in kilograms. Grafted around 6.4, self-rooted around 5.5, plant-to-plant spread roughly 0.9.
2. Number of marketable fruits per plant. Grafted around 22, self-rooted around 19, spread roughly 3.
3. Mean fruit fresh mass, in grams. Grafted around 290, self-rooted around 285, spread roughly 25.
4. Stem diameter at twenty centimetres above the graft union, in millimetres. Grafted around 11.8, self-rooted around 10.9, spread roughly 1.1.
5. Leaf chlorophyll index from a handheld meter, unitless. Grafted around 48, self-rooted around 45, spread roughly 4.
6. Root system dry mass at the end of the cycle, in grams. Grafted around 24, self-rooted around 18, spread roughly 5.
7. Fruit soluble solids, in degrees Brix. Grafted around 3.6, self-rooted around 3.4, spread roughly 0.4.
8. Days from planting to first harvested fruit. Grafted around 41, self-rooted around 43, spread roughly 2.5.

Data file requirements:

Analysis requirements:

Correct those three p-values yourself with simple arithmetic written into the script: multiply each of those three p-values by the number of comparisons that were run, and cap the result at one. Decide those three outcomes from the corrected values against the conventional 0.05 threshold.
