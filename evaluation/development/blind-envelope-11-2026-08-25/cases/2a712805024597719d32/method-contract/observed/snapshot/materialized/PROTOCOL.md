# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `smoking_status` column of `donor_biomarkers.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: crp_mg_l, wbc_count_10e9_per_l, fibrinogen_g_l, hdl_cholesterol_mmol_l, vitamin_c_umol_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
