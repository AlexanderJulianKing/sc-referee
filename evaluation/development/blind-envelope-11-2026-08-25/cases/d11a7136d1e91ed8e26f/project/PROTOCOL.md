# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `filter_status` column of `tap_water_survey.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: first_draw_lead_ug_l, flushed_lead_ug_l, first_draw_copper_mg_l, first_draw_turbidity_ntu.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
