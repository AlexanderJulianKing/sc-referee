# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `exposure_group` column of `shipyard_respiratory.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: fev1_litres, fvc_litres, feno_ppb, crp_mg_per_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
