# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `packing_media` column of `pilot_columns.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: bod_removal_percent, ammonium_nitrogen_removal_percent, effluent_suspended_solids_mg_per_l, biofilm_dry_mass_g_per_m2.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
