# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `diet` column of `trout_feeding_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: final_body_mass_g, specific_growth_rate_pct_per_day, feed_conversion_ratio, fillet_lipid_pct, hepatosomatic_index_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
