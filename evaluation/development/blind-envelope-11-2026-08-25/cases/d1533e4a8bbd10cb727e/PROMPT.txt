Study to implement:
An aquaculture feeding trial on rainbow trout. Eighty individually tagged juvenile trout were distributed across hatchery raceways and fed for twelve weeks. Forty fish received a conventional fishmeal-based diet and forty received a diet in which a large share of the fishmeal was replaced by insect meal; diet is the two-level grouping factor. Each fish was weighed and sampled individually at the end of the trial, and individual feed intake was recorded from tagged feeding records. Five outcome variables were declared in the trial plan before the trial started, and they are examined in the order below.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Final body mass, in grams. Fishmeal diet around 285, insect-meal diet around 272, individual spread roughly 35.
2. Specific growth rate, in percent of body mass per day. Fishmeal around 1.62, insect meal around 1.55, spread roughly 0.15.
3. Feed conversion ratio, unitless, feed eaten divided by mass gained. Fishmeal around 1.05, insect meal around 1.12, spread roughly 0.12.
4. Fillet lipid content, in percent of wet mass. Fishmeal around 8.2, insect meal around 7.6, spread roughly 1.1.
5. Hepatosomatic index, liver mass as a percent of body mass. Fishmeal around 1.35, insect meal around 1.28, spread roughly 0.2.
