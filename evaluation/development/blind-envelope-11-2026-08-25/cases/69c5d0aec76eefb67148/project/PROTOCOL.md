# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `processing_method` column of `pouch_shelf_life.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: colour_a_star, residual_ppo_activity_percent, aerobic_plate_count_log10_cfu_per_g.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
