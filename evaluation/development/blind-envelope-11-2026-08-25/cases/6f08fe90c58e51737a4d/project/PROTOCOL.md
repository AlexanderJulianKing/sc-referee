# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `substrate` column of `bsf_substrate_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: mean_larval_fresh_mass_mg, fresh_larval_yield_g, crude_protein_pct_dm, crude_fat_pct_dm, substrate_reduction_pct, development_time_days.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
