# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `landscape_type` column of `badger_landscape_data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: mean_nightly_distance_km, home_range_95_kernel_ha, body_condition_index, mean_time_active_hours, faecal_cortisol_ng_per_g.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
