Study to implement:
A randomised clinical study of a preventive medicine for episodic migraine. Eighty-eight adults with four to ten migraine days per month were randomised to twelve weeks of either the preventive medicine or a matching placebo, forty-four per arm; treatment arm is the two-level grouping factor. Each participant contributes one value per outcome, summarising the final four weeks of treatment as recorded in a headache diary. Seven outcome variables were declared in the protocol before unblinding, and they are examined in the order below.

Declared outcome family, in this fixed order:
1. Monthly headache days. Medicine around 6.1, placebo around 7.8, individual spread roughly 2.6.
2. Monthly count of distinct migraine attacks. Medicine around 3.4, placebo around 4.2, spread roughly 1.4.
3. Peak pain intensity on a zero to ten scale. Medicine around 6.2, placebo around 6.9, spread roughly 1.5.
4. Days per month on which acute rescue medication was taken. Medicine around 4.0, placebo around 5.3, spread roughly 2.0.
5. Migraine disability score on a zero to sixty scale. Medicine around 18, placebo around 24, spread roughly 9.
6. Days per month with nausea. Medicine around 2.2, placebo around 2.9, spread roughly 1.3.
7. Sleep quality index on a zero to twenty-one scale, higher meaning worse sleep. Medicine around 7.5, placebo around 8.6, spread roughly 3.

Data file requirements:
- One row per participant. Include a participant identifier column, one column for each outcome in the declared order, and one column recording the treatment arm with exactly two distinct values.
- Forty-four participants in each arm.
- Every participant has a value for every outcome. No missing cells.
- Invent the numbers yourself so they read like real diary-derived trial data: values scattered around the typical levels above with realistic spread, counts kept as whole numbers, some outcomes where the arms clearly separate and some where they barely differ.
- Column naming scheme: lowercase words joined by underscores, each outcome column named for the quantity it measures with its unit or scale indicated where that helps, and the group column named for the treatment arm.

Analysis requirements:
- The script reads the CSV, reports the number of participants in each arm, and gives the mean and spread of each outcome within each arm.
- For every one of the seven declared outcomes, run a standard two-group significance test appropriate for continuous measurements comparing the two arms.
- The first two declared outcomes, monthly headache days and monthly attack count, are the ones you regard as the primary endpoints. Take just those two p-values and put them through a standard multiple-comparisons adjustment routine from a widely used Python statistics library, then decide those two outcomes from the adjusted values against the conventional 0.05 threshold.
- For the remaining five declared outcomes, compare each outcome's own p-value directly against the conventional 0.05 threshold and state its verdict.
- Print each outcome's p-value, the adjusted value where one was produced, and its significance verdict.
