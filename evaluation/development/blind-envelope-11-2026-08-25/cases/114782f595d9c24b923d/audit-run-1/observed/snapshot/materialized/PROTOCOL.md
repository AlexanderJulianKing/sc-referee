# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `treatment_arm` column of `migraine_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: monthly_headache_days, monthly_migraine_attacks, peak_pain_intensity_0_10, rescue_medication_days_per_month, migraine_disability_score_0_60, nausea_days_per_month, sleep_quality_index_0_21.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
