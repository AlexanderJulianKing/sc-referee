# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `dbh_increment_cm` between the two levels of `treatment` (`unthinned` and `thinned`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the forest stand. The column `stand_code` carries the value that
identifies it: each forest stand has one distinct `stand_code` value. The thinning prescription was applied to the whole stand at once, so the stand is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `dbh_increment_cm` between the two levels of `treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
