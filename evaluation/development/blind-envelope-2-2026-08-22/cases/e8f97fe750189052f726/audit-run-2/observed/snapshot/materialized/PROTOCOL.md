# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `wing_centroid_size_mm` between the two levels of `diet` (`standard` and `high_sugar`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the rearing vial. The column `vial_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each rearing vial has one distinct `vial_id` value. The whole vial received one batch of medium and was assigned to one diet, so the vial is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `wing_centroid_size_mm` between the two levels of `diet`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
