# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `marketable_yield_g` between the two levels of `treatment` (`control` and `inoculated`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the plant. The column `plant_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each plant has one distinct `plant_id` value. Inoculation was applied to the individual plant at transplanting, so the plant is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `marketable_yield_g` between the two levels of `treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
