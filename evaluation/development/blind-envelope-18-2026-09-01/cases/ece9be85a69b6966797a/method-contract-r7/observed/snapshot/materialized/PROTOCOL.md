# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `medium` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: frond_number_increase, total_frond_area_mm2, chlorophyll_a_ug_per_g, mean_root_length_mm, dry_biomass_mg.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
