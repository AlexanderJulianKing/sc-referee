# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `stove_type` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: kitchen_pm25_ug_m3, kitchen_co_ppm, fuelwood_use_kg_day, respiratory_symptom_score, cooking_time_min.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
