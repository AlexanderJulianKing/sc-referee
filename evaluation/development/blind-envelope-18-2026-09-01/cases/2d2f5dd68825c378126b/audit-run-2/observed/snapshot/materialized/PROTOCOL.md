# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `thinning_intensity` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: fruit_weight_g, fruit_length_mm, fruit_width_mm, yield_per_palm_kg, total_soluble_solids_brix, fruit_moisture_pct, flesh_to_seed_ratio, fruit_firmness_n.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
