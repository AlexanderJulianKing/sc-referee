# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `disease_group` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: serum_ferritin_ug_l, serum_vitamin_b12_pmol_l, serum_folate_nmol_l, serum_zinc_umol_l, serum_25oh_vitamin_d_nmol_l, serum_magnesium_mmol_l.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
