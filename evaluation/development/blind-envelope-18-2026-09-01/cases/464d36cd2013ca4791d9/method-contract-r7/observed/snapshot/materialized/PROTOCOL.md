# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `protocol` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: pain_score_6h, rescue_analgesia_24h_mg, serum_cortisol_6h_ug_dl, heart_rate_6h_bpm, respiratory_rate_6h_brpm, food_intake_24h_g, rectal_temperature_6h_c.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
