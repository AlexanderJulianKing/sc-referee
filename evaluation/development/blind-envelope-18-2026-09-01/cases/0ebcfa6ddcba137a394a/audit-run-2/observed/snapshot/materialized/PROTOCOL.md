# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `sowing_time` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: grain_yield_t_ha, pods_per_plant, thousand_seed_weight_g, plant_height_cm, seed_protein_pct, chocolate_spot_severity_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
