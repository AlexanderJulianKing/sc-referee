# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `site_lighting` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_mass_mg, cephalothorax_width_mm, web_capture_area_cm2, mesh_width_mm, prey_items.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
