# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `street_type` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: indoor_night_level_db, facade_lden_db, sleep_disturbance_score, awakenings_per_night.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
