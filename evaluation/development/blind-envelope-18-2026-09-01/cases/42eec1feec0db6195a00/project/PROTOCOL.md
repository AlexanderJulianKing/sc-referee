# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `pit_soil_type` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: trunk_diameter_increment_mm, canopy_area_m2, leaf_chlorophyll_index, midday_stem_water_potential_mpa.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
