# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `harvest_stage` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: fresh_inflorescence_biomass_g, oil_yield_pct, linalool_pct, linalyl_acetate_pct, camphor_pct, cineole_1_8_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
