# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `application_method` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: fev1_l, feno_ppb, airway_symptom_score, peak_tvoc_mg_m3, eye_skin_irritation_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
