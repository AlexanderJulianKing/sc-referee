# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `region` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: peroxide_value_meq_o2_kg, free_fatty_acids_pct, total_tocopherols_mg_kg, oxidative_stability_index_h, chlorophyll_pigments_mg_kg, erucic_acid_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
