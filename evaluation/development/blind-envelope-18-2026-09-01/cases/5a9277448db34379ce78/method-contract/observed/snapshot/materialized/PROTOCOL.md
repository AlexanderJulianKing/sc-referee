# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `liquid_thickness` column of `data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: penetration_aspiration_score, mealtime_duration_min, daily_oral_fluid_intake_ml, meal_completion_pct, weight_change_kg, coughing_episodes_per_meal.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
