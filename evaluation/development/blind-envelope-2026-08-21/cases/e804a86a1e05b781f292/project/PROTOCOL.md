# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `final_colony_mass_g` between the two levels of `pollen_diet` (`monofloral` and `mixed`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the microcolony. The column `hive_label` carries the value that
identifies it: each microcolony has one distinct `hive_label` value. Each colony lived alone in its own box and was fed on its own, and the diet was assigned per colony, so the colony is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `final_colony_mass_g` between the two levels of `pollen_diet`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
