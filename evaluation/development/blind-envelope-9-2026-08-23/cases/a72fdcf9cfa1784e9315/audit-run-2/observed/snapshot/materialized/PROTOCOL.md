# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `polychaete_count` between the two levels of `station_group` (`footprint` and `reference`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the sampling station. The column `station_ref` carries the value that
identifies it: each sampling station has one distinct `station_ref` value. The station group is a property of the station and holds for all five grabs taken at that station, so the sampling station is the unit that carries a group label.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `polychaete_count` between the two levels of `station_group`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
