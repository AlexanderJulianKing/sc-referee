# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `soluble_solids_brix` between the two levels of `treatment` (`inoculated` and `uninoculated`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the bush. The column `bush_label` carries the value that
identifies it: each bush has one distinct `bush_label` value. Each bush was inoculated or left uninoculated as a whole at planting and five clusters were picked from it carrying that treatment label, so the bush is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `soluble_solids_brix` between the two levels of `treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
