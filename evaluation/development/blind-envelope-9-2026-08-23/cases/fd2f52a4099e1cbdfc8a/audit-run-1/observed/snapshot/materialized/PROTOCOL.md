# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `reaction_time_ms` between the two levels of `training_regime` (`adaptive` and `active_control`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the volunteer. The column `volunteer_ref` carries the value that
identifies it: each volunteer has one distinct `volunteer_ref` value. Each volunteer was assigned as a whole to one regime and performed twelve trials carrying that regime label, so the volunteer is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `reaction_time_ms` between the two levels of `training_regime`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
