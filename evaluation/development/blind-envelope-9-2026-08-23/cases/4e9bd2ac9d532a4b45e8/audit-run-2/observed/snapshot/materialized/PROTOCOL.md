# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `bmd_g_per_cm2` between the two levels of `supplement_regime` (`vitamin_d_calcium` and `vitamin_d_only`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the woman. The column `patient_ref` carries the value that
identifies it: each woman has one distinct `patient_ref` value. Each woman completed one of the two supplement regimes as a whole and contributed four vertebral-level readings from her one scan carrying that regime label, so the woman is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `bmd_g_per_cm2` between the two levels of `supplement_regime`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
