# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `fibre_diameter_um` between the two levels of `diet_group` (`supplemented` and `unsupplemented`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the alpaca. The column `alpaca_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each alpaca has one distinct `alpaca_id` value. Each alpaca received one of the two rations and was sampled in all four monthly samplings, so the alpaca is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `fibre_diameter_um` between the two levels of `diet_group`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
