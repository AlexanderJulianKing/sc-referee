# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `body_mass_mg` between the two levels of `treatment` (`biochar` and `control`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the soil mesocosm. The column `mesocosm_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each soil mesocosm has one distinct `mesocosm_id` value. Each mesocosm was assigned at random to one of the two treatments and ten worms were removed from it and weighed, so the mesocosm is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `body_mass_mg` between the two levels of `treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
