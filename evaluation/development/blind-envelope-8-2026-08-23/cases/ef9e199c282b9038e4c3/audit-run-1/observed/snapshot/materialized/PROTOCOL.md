# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `bout_duration_s` between the two levels of `noise_condition` (`noise` and `quiet`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the bird. The column `bird_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each bird has one distinct `bird_id` value. Each bird was housed in one of the two conditions and had twelve song bouts recorded under it, so the bird is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `bout_duration_s` between the two levels of `noise_condition`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
