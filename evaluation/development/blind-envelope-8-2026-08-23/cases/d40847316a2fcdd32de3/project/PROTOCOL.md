# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `total_nitrogen_pct` between the two levels of `burn_block` (`burned` and `unburned`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the termite mound. The column `mound_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each termite mound has one distinct `mound_id` value. The burn block is a property of the mound and holds for all eight soil cores taken at that mound, so the mound is the unit that carries a group label.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `total_nitrogen_pct` between the two levels of `burn_block`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
