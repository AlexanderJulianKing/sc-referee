# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `flush_yield_g` between the two levels of `substrate` (`supplemented` and `standard`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the growing chamber. The column `chamber_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each growing chamber has one distinct `chamber_id` value. Each chamber was filled with one of the two substrates and kept that substrate for all four of its flushes, so the chamber is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `flush_yield_g` between the two levels of `substrate`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
