This water quality dataset presents dissolved oxygen measurements collected from two river monitoring locations over a seven-day sampling period in February 2024. The upstream station serves as a reference point for assessing baseline conditions in relatively pristine water, while the downstream station is positioned to detect any water quality degradation. Each record represents a single discrete water sample collected by automated monitoring equipment. Measurements include dissolved oxygen concentration (DO in mg/L), water pH, temperature (°C), and collection date. The analysis examines whether dissolved oxygen concentrations significantly differ between the two locations, which would indicate potential environmental stressors affecting aquatic oxygen availability downstream.

Independent unit column: sample_id
