This dataset contains cognitive response time measurements from a study examining the relationship between regular exercise and cognitive processing speed. Twenty participants completed a computerized reaction time task while their performance was measured. Participants are classified into two independent groups based on self-reported exercise frequency: those with regular exercise (at least 3 times per week) and those without regular exercise (less than 1 time per week). Response times are recorded in milliseconds. Each row represents one participant's mean reaction time across multiple test trials. Independent unit column: participant_id
