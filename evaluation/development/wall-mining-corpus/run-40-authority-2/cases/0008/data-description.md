## Seed Weight Comparison Study

This dataset contains measurements of individual bean seeds from two distinct varieties: heritage (traditional landraces) and modern (contemporary commercial cultivars). Each row represents one seed specimen with its unique identifier, varietal classification, and dry weight in milligrams.

Heritage beans represent heirloom varieties maintained through traditional breeding practices, while modern beans come from intensive commercial breeding programs. Seed weight is a critical agronomic trait affecting germination speed, seedling vigor, and field establishment. This comparison assesses morphological differences between heritage and modern germplasm by analyzing variation in seed size between the two groups.

The twenty seeds were sampled from a single growing season to control for environmental effects on seed development and maturation.

Independent unit column: seed_id
