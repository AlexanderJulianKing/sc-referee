This dataset contains seed germination time measurements from a controlled botanical experiment comparing two light wavelength treatments. Seeds from a single radish variety were exposed to either red (650 nm) or blue (450 nm) wavelength light in identical growth chambers maintained at 22°C with 16-hour photoperiods. Germination time was recorded as the number of days from seed planting until root emergence (radicle protrusion ≥1 mm). The experiment represents a straightforward comparison of two independent treatment groups with continuous outcome measurements. Each observation is a single seed subjected to one of the two light treatments, providing independent replicates for statistical comparison.
Independent unit column: seed_id
