Water treatment efficiency study comparing residual chlorine levels between two disinfection methods. Water samples (n=20) were collected from municipal treatment plant outlets and analyzed using standard photometric techniques. Two treatment processes were evaluated: Standard conventional treatment and Advanced enhanced treatment. Residual chlorine concentration (measured in ppm) was the primary outcome variable, with measurements validated against acceptable operational ranges (0–5 ppm). The study design involves independent samples from each treatment method, allowing for direct statistical comparison of disinfection efficacy.
