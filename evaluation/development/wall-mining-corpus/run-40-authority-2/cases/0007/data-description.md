This dataset contains reaction time measurements from a randomized controlled trial investigating the effects of caffeine on cognitive processing speed. Thirty participants were recruited and randomly assigned to two equal groups: a control group receiving a placebo capsule and a caffeine group receiving 100mg of caffeine. After treatment administration, each participant completed a standardized visual reaction time task consisting of detecting stimulus onset and responding by button press. Reaction times in milliseconds represent the mean response latency across trials for each participant. The study examined whether caffeine administration produces detectable changes in the speed of cognitive processing as measured by this laboratory task.

Independent unit column: participant_id
