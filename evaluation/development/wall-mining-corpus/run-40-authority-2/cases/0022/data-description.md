This dataset contains stem height measurements from a controlled botanical experiment testing soil pH effects on plant growth. Seedlings of a common legume species were randomly assigned to either neutral pH soil (control, n=8) or acidic soil (treatment, n=8) and grown for 4 weeks under identical light, temperature, and moisture conditions.

**Columns:**
- `plant_id`: Unique plant identifier (e.g., P001, P002)
- `soil_pH`: Treatment condition (neutral for pH ~7, acidic for pH ~5)
- `stem_height_cm`: Final stem height measurement in centimeters at the end of the 4-week growing period

The study examines whether soil pH influences plant stem elongation in seedlings. All plants were grown in hydroponic chambers with identical nutrient solutions, differing only in the pH adjustment of the growing medium.

Independent unit column: plant_id
