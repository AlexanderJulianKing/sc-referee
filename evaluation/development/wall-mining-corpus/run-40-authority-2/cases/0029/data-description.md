This dataset contains heavy metal concentrations measured in sediment cores collected from two distinct coastal regions during June 2025. The industrial coastal region experiences significant shipping traffic and industrial manufacturing activities, while the pristine region is a designated marine protected area with minimal anthropogenic disturbance. Sediment samples were extracted at three depths (5, 10, and 15 meters) to assess vertical distribution patterns. Heavy metal concentrations (mercury, lead, and cadmium) are quantified in micrograms per kilogram (µg/kg) of dry sediment weight. The analysis focuses on mercury as the primary bioaccumulative pollutant of environmental concern. Eight replicate samples from each region provide sufficient power for detecting meaningful differences in contaminant levels between the two coastal environments.

Independent unit column: sample_id
