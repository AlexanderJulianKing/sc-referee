A study examining sleep quality among knowledge workers with different work arrangements. Participants completed a sleep quality questionnaire (0-100 scale) and provided demographic information. The dataset includes 16 workers: 8 remote workers and 8 office workers.

Variables:
- worker_id: Unique identifier for each participant
- work_type: Work arrangement category (remote or office)
- sleep_quality_score: Self-reported sleep quality on 0-100 scale
- age: Participant age in years
- years_in_role: Years in current work arrangement
- hours_per_night: Average nightly sleep duration

Independent unit column: worker_id
