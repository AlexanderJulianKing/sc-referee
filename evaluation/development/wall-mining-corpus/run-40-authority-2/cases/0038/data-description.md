This experiment measured plant heights after 60 days of growth under two different light conditions: LED supplemental lighting or natural sunlight only. Each row represents one plant that was randomly assigned to one of the two treatment conditions. The plant_id uniquely identifies each plant specimen. The light_treatment column indicates whether the plant received LED lighting ("LED") or was grown under natural light only ("Natural"). Height measurements were recorded in centimeters and stored in the height_cm column. All plants were measured on day 60 to allow consistent comparison across the treatment groups. This study tests whether supplemental LED lighting produces significantly different growth outcomes compared to natural light conditions alone. Independent unit column: plant_id
