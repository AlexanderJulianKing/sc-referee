This dataset contains daily dissolved oxygen measurements and water quality parameters collected from two river sampling sites. The upstream site serves as a reference location representing undisturbed conditions, while the downstream site is located below a potential point source affecting water quality. Water samples were collected once daily for 10 consecutive days (January 15-24, 2026) at each location. Measured parameters include dissolved oxygen concentration (mg/L), pH, and water temperature (°C). Each sample represents a discrete water collection event at a specific site on a specific date. The dataset is used to perform statistical comparison of oxygen dynamics between the reference and downstream locations to assess potential water quality degradation.
Independent unit column: sample_id
