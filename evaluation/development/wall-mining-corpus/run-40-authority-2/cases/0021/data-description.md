This dataset contains photosynthetic efficiency measurements (Fv/Fm ratio) from plant specimens grown under two distinct treatment conditions: control (adequate water supply) and drought stress. The Fv/Fm value represents the maximum quantum yield of photosystem II, a fundamental measure derived from chlorophyll fluorescence analysis. Values range from 0 to 1, with higher values indicating superior photosynthetic performance and plant health. Twenty plants were randomly assigned to either the control or drought treatment group and maintained for eight weeks. At the conclusion of the experiment, a single measurement of photosynthetic efficiency was recorded from each plant using a portable fluorometer at midday under standardized light conditions. This experimental design allows for direct comparison of how water stress affects the photochemical efficiency of photosynthesis.

Independent unit column: plant_id
