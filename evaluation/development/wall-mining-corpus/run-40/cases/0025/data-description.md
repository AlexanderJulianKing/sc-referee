Municipal waste management contamination study tracking material purity in recycling streams across different collection methods over five weeks. Each row represents a weekly quality audit sample from one collection approach: residential curbside bins, commercial dumpsters, public drop-off centers, or scheduled curbside pickup days. Auditors sorted 500 items from each collection point and counted non-recyclable materials and misplaced items as contamination. Contamination_Rate is the proportion of contaminated items in each sample. Higher contamination rates increase municipal processing costs and reduce recovered material quality for end markets.
