Stream water quality monitoring data collected at six time points over six months (January-June 2024) from three monitoring stations representing different reaches of a river system. Each row contains a single measurement event at a specific location. Five water quality parameters were measured: pH (acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale, neutral at 7), dissolved oxygen or DO (mg/L, essential for aquatic life), water temperature (Celsius), turbidity (Nephelometric Turbidity Units measuring cloudiness), and phosphate concentration (mg/L, a nutrient indicator). Dataset comprises 18 total observations enabling analysis of seasonal trends and inter-station differences in water quality conditions.
