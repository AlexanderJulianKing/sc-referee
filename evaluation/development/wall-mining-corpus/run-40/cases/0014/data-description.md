## River Water Quality Monitoring Dataset

Weekly water quality measurements collected from three monitoring stations along a river system from July to August 2026. Each station represents a different longitudinal position: Upstream (reference point with minimal development), Midstream (agricultural region), and Downstream (developed area).

**Parameters measured:**
- **pH**: Acidity/alkalinity (scale 0-14, 7 is neutral)
- **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)**: Measured in mg/L, critical for aquatic ecosystem health
- **Temperature**: Water temperature in degrees Celsius
- **Turbidity**: Water clarity measured in Nephelometric Turbidity Units (NTU)
- **Nitrates**: Concentration in mg/L, indicator of agricultural and sewage runoff

**Data characteristics:**
- 21 total observations (3 stations × 7 weekly surveys)
- Time period: July 1 - August 12, 2026
- Real-world patterns: Downstream water shows elevation in nitrates and lower dissolved oxygen relative to upstream, consistent with cumulative anthropogenic impact
