## Water Quality Monitoring Dataset

### Overview
This dataset contains weekly water quality measurements from three monitoring stations along a river system over a 52-week period (one complete year). The data tracks four critical water quality parameters essential for environmental health assessment and regulatory compliance.

### Measurement Sites
- **North_Fork**: Upstream site in relatively undisturbed forest habitat; baseline reference location
- **Main_Channel**: Central monitoring point where multiple tributaries converge; higher population impact
- **South_Tributary**: Agricultural runoff area; influenced by seasonal farming practices

### Parameters Measured

**pH**: Acidity/alkalinity level (dimensionless); normal freshwater range 6.5-8.5

**Dissolved Oxygen (DO)**: Milligrams per liter; critical for aquatic life; minimum healthy level ≥5.0 mg/L

**Turbidity**: Nephelometric turbidity units (NTU); measure of water clarity/suspended solids; standard ≤1.0 NTU

**Nitrate**: Milligrams per liter; nutrient indicator and agricultural pollution marker; standard ≤10 mg/L

### Data Collection Method
Samples collected weekly on consistent days using standard USGS protocols. All measurements performed in certified laboratory within 24 hours of collection. Equipment calibrated daily.

### Data Characteristics
- 156 total observations (52 weeks × 3 sites)
- No missing values
- North_Fork exhibits stable, compliant water quality throughout year
- Main_Channel shows elevated turbidity and moderate nitrate from urban/industrial influence
- South_Tributary demonstrates increasing nitrate trend from spring/summer agricultural runoff patterns

### Regulatory Context
Data evaluated against EPA secondary drinking water standards and aquatic life protection criteria. Compliance assessed by percentage of measurements within established threshold ranges across all four parameters.
