# Water Quality Monitoring Dataset

This dataset contains chemical and physical measurements from three aquatic monitoring sites collected over a three-month period (June through August 2024). Samples were collected bi-weekly at each location.

## Sites
- **North Pond**: Small impoundment in agricultural area
- **South Pond**: Municipal park retention pond
- **East Lake**: Natural freshwater lake system

## Parameters Measured
- **pH**: Acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale; optimal range 6.5-8.5 for aquatic life
- **Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L)**: Critical for fish respiration; minimum threshold 5.0 mg/L
- **Nitrate (mg/L)**: Macronutrient indicating nitrogen availability and potential eutrophication
- **Phosphate (mg/L)**: Limiting nutrient in freshwater systems; elevated levels trigger algal blooms
- **Temperature (°C)**: Affects metabolic rates, oxygen solubility, and nutrient cycling

## Data Quality
15 total observations across 3 sites and 5 sampling dates. No missing values. All measurements conducted using calibrated field instruments with quality control procedures.
