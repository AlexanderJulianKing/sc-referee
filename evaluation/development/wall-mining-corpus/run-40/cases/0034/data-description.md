## Acoustic Environmental Monitoring Dataset

This dataset contains 40 ambient sound measurements collected at 15-minute intervals throughout a single workday (8:00 AM to 5:45 PM) across four distinct office zones: Open Office (shared collaborative workspace), Conference Room (enclosed meeting spaces), and Breakroom (employee rest area).

**Measurement Specifications:**
- Sound pressure levels recorded in decibels (dB A-weighted)
- Calibrated sound level meter positioned at fixed 1.2m height
- Measurements include background acoustic environment during typical office operations

**Geographic Variation:**
Expected patterns reflect architectural acoustic properties. The Conference Room demonstrates lowest noise levels (51-58 dB) due to door closure and absorptive surfaces. The Breakroom exhibits highest levels (64-73 dB) associated with employee conversations, equipment use, and increased activity. Open Office shows intermediate values reflecting variable occupancy and conversation dynamics.

**Temporal Pattern:**
Noise levels show diurnal variation with peak intensity during midday lunch period (12:00-13:45), corresponding with maximum occupancy and social activity. Morning hours (8-10 AM) and late afternoon (17:00+) show relatively lower acoustic energy, reflecting reduced staff density.
