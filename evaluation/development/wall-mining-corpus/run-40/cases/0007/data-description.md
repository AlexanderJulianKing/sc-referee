## Soil Quality Data Description

This dataset contains measurements from 10 soil samples collected from agricultural fields representing three major soil texture classifications. Each sample was analyzed for key chemical and physical properties that indicate soil fertility and suitability for crop production.

### Fields

- **sample_id**: Unique identifier for each soil sample (S001 through S010)
- **soil_type**: Textural classification of soil (loamy, sandy, or clayey) based on particle size distribution
- **pH**: Acidity/alkalinity measurement on a scale of 0-14 (7 is neutral; values below 7 are acidic, above 7 are alkaline)
- **nitrogen**: Total nitrogen content expressed as percentage by weight (range 0-5%, critical for plant growth)
- **phosphorus**: Available phosphorus content as percentage by weight (range 0-3%, essential for root development)
- **potassium**: Available potassium content as percentage by weight (range 0-2%, important for plant metabolism)
- **organic_matter**: Soil organic matter content as percentage by weight (range 0-5%, affects water retention and fertility)

### Sample Characteristics

Samples represent three distinct soil types: loamy soils (samples 1-3 and 10) considered optimal for most crops due to balanced particle composition, sandy soils (samples 4-6) characterized by coarse particles that drain rapidly but retain fewer nutrients, and clayey soils (samples 7-9) with small particles providing high nutrient content but slower drainage. All measurements are from topsoil within the top 20 cm, the agronomically most significant layer where crop roots concentrate nutrient uptake.
