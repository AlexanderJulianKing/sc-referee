# Water Quality Monitoring Dataset

This dataset contains water quality measurements from three monitoring stations (North, South, and East) collected across four consecutive days in August 2026. Each observation includes five water quality parameters measured at regular monitoring intervals.

## Parameters

- **pH**: Acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale; optimal range 6.5-8.5 for freshwater ecosystems
- **Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L)**: Critical for aquatic life; minimum threshold 5.0 mg/L
- **Turbidity (NTU)**: Measures water clarity; values above 5 NTU indicate excessive suspended solids
- **Nitrate (mg/L)**: Nutrient level indicating potential contamination from agriculture or urban runoff; threshold 10 mg/L
- **Temperature (°C)**: Water temperature affecting biological activity and oxygen solubility

## Collection Context

The three stations monitor different reaches of an urban waterway. North Station is upstream in a residential area, South Station is midstream near agricultural land, and East Station is downstream in an industrial zone. Daily measurements enable detection of acute contamination events and assessment of ecosystem health against environmental quality standards.
