Pollen grain diameter measurements in micrometers for four plant species collected from fresh botanical specimens. Data derived from scanning electron microscopy imagery of fully mature pollen grains at peak germination viability. Each species has five replicate measurements. Species measured: Rosa (wild rose), Lilium (Easter lily), Quercus (white oak), and Pinus (eastern white pine). These measurements serve as morphological descriptors for taxonomic identification and represent naturally occurring variation within species collections.
