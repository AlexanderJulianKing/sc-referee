Water quality measurements from four monitoring stations collected at three time intervals spanning January to March 2026. Each record represents a single field survey containing pH level (acidity), dissolved oxygen concentration in mg/L (indicator of biological activity and pollution stress), turbidity in NTU (suspended particle content), and water temperature in Celsius. Stations S001 and S004 show pristine conditions while S002 and S003 exhibit moderate to high turbidity suggesting sediment loading or algal activity.
