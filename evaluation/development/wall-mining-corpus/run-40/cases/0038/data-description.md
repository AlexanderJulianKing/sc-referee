## Water Quality Monitoring Dataset

This dataset contains water quality measurements from four sampling locations in an aquatic ecosystem, collected across three sampling dates in August 2026.

### Variables

- **location**: Name of the sampling site (North Cove, South Inlet, Middle Deep, East Shallows)
- **date**: Sampling date in YYYY-MM-DD format
- **depth_m**: Water depth at which measurement was taken, in meters
- **temperature_c**: Water temperature in degrees Celsius
- **ph**: pH value (7 = neutral, <7 acidic, >7 alkaline)
- **dissolved_oxygen_mg_l**: Dissolved oxygen concentration in milligrams per liter
- **conductivity_us_cm**: Electrical conductivity in microSiemens per centimeter (indicates dissolved mineral content)

### Context

Water quality parameters indicate ecosystem health. Temperature affects oxygen solubility and biological activity. pH influences nutrient availability and organism survival. Dissolved oxygen is essential for aquatic life. Conductivity reflects ion concentration and watershed geology.

The dataset contains 15 observations across 4 locations with multiple depths and temporal coverage.
