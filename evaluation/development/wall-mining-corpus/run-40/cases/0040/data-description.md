## Soil Sample Data

Agricultural soil samples from 15 field plots, each analyzed for pH and macronutrient content. Samples were collected from the surface layer (0–6 inches) using standard procedures and represent diverse soil types across a farming operation.

**Variables:**
- **Plot_ID**: Unique plot identifier (A1–E3 nomenclature)
- **pH**: Soil acidity/alkalinity on 0–14 scale; optimal 6.0–7.5 for most crops
- **Nitrogen_ppm**: Plant-available nitrogen in parts per million; target 20–40 ppm for adequate growth
- **Phosphorus_ppm**: Available phosphorus; target 15–30 ppm for root development and energy
- **Potassium_ppm**: Available potassium; target 150–250 ppm for overall plant vigor
- **Organic_Matter_percent**: Percentage of organic matter by weight; target 3–6% for soil structure and water retention
- **Soil_Texture**: Particle size classification (sandy, sandy loam, loam, clay loam, clay)

Data reflects realistic field conditions with mixed soil types, varying nutrient levels, and different management histories across plots.
