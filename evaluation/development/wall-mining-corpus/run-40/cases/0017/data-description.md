Water quality monitoring data from five stream sites with measurements taken over three consecutive weeks. Each measurement includes pH, turbidity (NTU), dissolved oxygen (mg/L), electrical conductivity (µS/cm), and the percentage of the contributing catchment that is urbanized. Sites S001 and S003 drain predominantly natural areas with low urbanization, while S002 and S004 drain highly urbanized catchments. Site S005 represents a mixed urban-rural scenario. The analysis investigates whether urbanization drives systematic changes in water quality signatures.
