## Bird Survey Dataset

This dataset documents avian observations recorded at a suburban residential monitoring site over five consecutive months spanning late winter through late spring (January–May). Each observation record specifies the month, identified bird species, and count of individual birds observed during a standardized 30-minute survey period.

The survey captured both year-round resident species such as Black-capped Chickadees and Mourning Doves, as well as migratory species whose abundance increases noticeably during spring months (March–May). The American Robin, which exhibits strong seasonal presence variation, is the most frequently observed species overall. The data reflects natural ecological patterns where temperate bird communities expand in species composition and individual density as breeding season approaches and migrating species return from wintering grounds.

Total records: 29 observations across 5 months and 9 unique species.
