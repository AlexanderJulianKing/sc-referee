Water quality measurements from three intensive aquaculture tanks monitored over weekly intervals. Each row represents one measurement event containing five variables: tank identifier (1-3), pH level indicating acidity-alkalinity, water temperature in degrees Celsius, dissolved oxygen concentration in mg/L, and total ammonia nitrogen in ppm. Target ranges for most cultured fish species: pH 7.0–7.5, temperature 23–26°C, dissolved oxygen >6 mg/L, and ammonia <0.5 ppm. Data reflects typical variation in recirculating production systems with different stocking densities and management practices across tanks.
