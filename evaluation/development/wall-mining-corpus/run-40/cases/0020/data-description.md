## Water Quality Monitoring Dataset

This dataset contains 16 weekly measurements of water quality parameters from 4 sampling sites in a freshwater lake during July 2026. Each observation records six variables:

- **site**: Sampling location (North Bay, Central Deep, South Inlet, West Tributary)
- **date**: Collection date (weekly intervals)
- **ph**: Acidity/alkalinity on the pH scale (0-14, neutral at 7)
- **do_mg_l**: Dissolved oxygen concentration in milligrams per liter
- **temp_c**: Water temperature in degrees Celsius
- **turbidity_ntu**: Cloudiness in Nephelometric Turbidity Units
- **conductivity_us_cm**: Electrical conductivity in microSiemens per centimeter

The sites represent distinct lake regions: North Bay (shallow, well-oxygenated near shore), Central Deep (profundal zone with stratification), South Inlet (river discharge influence), and West Tributary (tributary stream input). These parameters are standard aquatic health indicators used to detect pollution, eutrophication, and ecosystem stress.
