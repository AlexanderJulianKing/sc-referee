# Soil Quality and Crop Yield Dataset

This dataset comprises soil nutrient measurements and grain crop yields from 12 agricultural fields in a temperate farming region. Each record represents a single field's soil analysis conducted at planting time and its corresponding harvest yield.

## Variables

- **field_id**: Unique field identifier (F001–F012)
- **soil_ph**: Soil acidity measurement on 14-point scale (typical range 5.7–7.1); affects nutrient availability
- **nitrogen_ppm**: Plant-available nitrogen in parts per million; primary macronutrient for growth
- **phosphorus_ppm**: Soil phosphorus concentration; essential for root development and energy transfer
- **potassium_ppm**: Soil potassium level; regulates water and mineral transport
- **yield_kg_per_hectare**: Harvested grain yield in kilograms per hectare; agronomic outcome measure

## Data Characteristics

- 12 fields representative of regional farming practices
- Soil tests conducted using standard agricultural laboratory methods
- Yield measured at commercial harvest
- Realistic natural variation in soil chemistry and productivity
- No missing values
