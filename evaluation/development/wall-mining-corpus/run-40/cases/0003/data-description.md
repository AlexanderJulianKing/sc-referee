## Water Quality Dataset

Monthly water quality measurements from a river system collected over six months (January through June 2024) at three sampling locations: upstream, midstream, and downstream. Each location was sampled once per month.

**Measurement Parameters:**
- **pH**: Acidity/alkalinity (unitless, scale 0-14)
- **temperature_c**: Water temperature in Celsius
- **dissolved_oxygen_mg_l**: Dissolved oxygen concentration in mg/L, critical for aquatic life
- **turbidity_ntu**: Water clarity in Nephelometric Turbidity Units
- **phosphorus_mg_l**: Nutrient concentration in mg/L
- **nitrate_mg_l**: Nitrogen compound concentration in mg/L

**Sampling Design:** 
18 total observations (3 locations × 6 months). Downstream location typically shows higher turbidity due to sediment accumulation and increased nutrient levels, while upstream maintains cleaner conditions. Temperature increases seasonally.
