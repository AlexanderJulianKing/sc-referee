Dataset contains water quality measurements from four monitoring sites collected over three dates in August 2026. Parameters tracked include pH (acidity, optimal 6.5–7.5), dissolved oxygen in mg/L (critical for aquatic organisms; >5 mg/L generally required), turbidity in NTU (sediment and cloudiness; lower is clearer), and nitrate in mg/L (indicator of nutrient loading and potential pollution). Sites represent different water bodies or river sections with varying environmental conditions and pollution pressures.
