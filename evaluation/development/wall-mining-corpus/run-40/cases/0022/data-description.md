## Steel Hardness Production Data

Rockwell hardness (HRC scale) measurements from four production batches at a steel manufacturing facility. Each batch contains 7-8 samples measured using standardized testing procedures. Measurements range from 58.2 to 62.5 HRC.

**Purpose:** Quality assessment to identify whether hardness varies significantly across batches and to diagnose potential process control issues or equipment drift in the heat treatment process.

**Variables:**
- `batch_id`: Production batch identifier (Batch_A through Batch_D)
- `hardness_value`: Rockwell hardness reading in HRC units
