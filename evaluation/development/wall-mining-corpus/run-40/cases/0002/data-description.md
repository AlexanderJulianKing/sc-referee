Seedling height measurements (in cm) recorded across three light treatment conditions: low, medium, and high intensity. Heights were measured on days 0, 7, 14, and 21 of a controlled growth experiment. Each row represents a single height measurement at a specific time point and treatment level. The dataset contains 12 observations total, with 4 measurements per treatment group. This data is used to assess whether different light intensities produce significantly different growth rates and final plant heights.
