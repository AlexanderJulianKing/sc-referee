## Crop Yield Study Data

This dataset contains observations from 20 agricultural field plots monitored during a single growing season. Each plot represents a distinct management zone within a larger farming operation.

**Variables measured:**

- **plot_id**: Unique identifier for each field plot (P001 through P020)
- **fertilizer_rate**: Nitrogen fertilizer application rate in kilograms per hectare, ranging from 100 to 175 kg/ha
- **rainfall**: Total seasonal precipitation in millimeters, ranging from 380 to 640 mm
- **temperature**: Average growing season temperature in degrees Celsius, ranging from 21.2 to 24.5°C
- **yield**: Final crop yield measured in kilograms per hectare, ranging from 3650 to 5580 kg/ha

**Data characteristics:**

The data represents a naturalistic observational study where fertilizer application, rainfall, and temperature vary naturally across plots. Environmental conditions (rainfall and temperature) reflect spatial heterogeneity across the farm, while fertilizer rates represent deliberate management decisions. Yield observations are actual harvested production figures. No plots received experimental treatments; this is production data from routine farm operations. Sample size is 20 plots, sufficient for basic correlation and regression analysis but not for complex multivariate modeling.
