Soil samples collected from three garden plots (A, B, C) at multiple depths (10, 20, and 30 cm below surface). Each measurement records soil pH, macronutrient concentrations (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium in mg/kg), organic matter percentage, and soil moisture percentage. This represents a typical experimental dataset from an agricultural soil assessment study designed to evaluate nutrient availability across different plot locations and soil profiles. Variation in soil properties with depth reflects natural stratification and microbial activity patterns commonly observed in cultivated soils.
