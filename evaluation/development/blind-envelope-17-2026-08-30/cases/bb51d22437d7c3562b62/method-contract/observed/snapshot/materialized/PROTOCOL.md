# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `feed_group` column of `axolotl_feed_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: specific_growth_rate_pct_per_day, final_body_mass_g, feed_conversion_ratio, whole_body_lipid_pct, cortisol_release_ng_per_l_per_h.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
