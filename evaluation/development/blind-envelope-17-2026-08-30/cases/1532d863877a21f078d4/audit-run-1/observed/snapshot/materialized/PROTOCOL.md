# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `seed_treatment` column of `sunflower_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: plant_height_cm, head_diameter_cm, filled_seed_number, thousand_seed_mass_g, seed_oil_content_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
