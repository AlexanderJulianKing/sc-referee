# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `sleep_study_data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: sleep_onset_latency_min, wake_after_sleep_onset_min, total_sleep_time_min, sleep_efficiency_pct, insomnia_severity_index_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
