# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `curing_method` column of `vanilla_curing_pods.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: vanillin_g_per_100g, moisture_pct, p_hydroxybenzaldehyde_mg_per_100g, bend_force_n, surface_lightness_l_star.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
