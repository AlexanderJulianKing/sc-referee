# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `diet` column of `parrot_diet_data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: plasma_retinol_ug_dl, body_mass_g, plasma_calcium_mmol_l, feather_condition_score.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
