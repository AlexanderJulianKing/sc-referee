# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `application_method` column of `exposure_data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: urinary_dap_ug_per_g_creatinine, cholinesterase_pct_baseline, dermal_deposition_ug_per_hand, inhalation_pad_ug, symptom_score_0_20.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
