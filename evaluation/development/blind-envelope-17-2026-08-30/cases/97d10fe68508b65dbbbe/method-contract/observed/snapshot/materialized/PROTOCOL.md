# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `substrate` column of `mussel_rearing_data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: shell_length_increment_mm, wet_mass_gain_g, condition_index_pct, foot_glycogen_mg_per_g, clearance_rate_l_per_h.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
