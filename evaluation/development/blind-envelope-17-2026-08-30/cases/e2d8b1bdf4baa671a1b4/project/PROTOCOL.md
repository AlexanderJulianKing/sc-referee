# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `temperature_c` column of `daphnia_temperature.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: age_first_brood_days, body_length_day14_mm, offspring_day21, heart_rate_day10_bpm.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
