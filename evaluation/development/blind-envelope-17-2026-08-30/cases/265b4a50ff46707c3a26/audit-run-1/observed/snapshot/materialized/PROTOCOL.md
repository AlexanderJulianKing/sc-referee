# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `aphasia_therapy_data.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: naming_accuracy_pct, speech_rate_wpm, functional_communication_0_100.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
