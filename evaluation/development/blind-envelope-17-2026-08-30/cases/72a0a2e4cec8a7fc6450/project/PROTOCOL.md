# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `group` column of `levothyroxine_formulation_trial.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: tsh_miu_l, free_t4_pmol_l, total_cholesterol_mmol_l, symptom_score_0_40.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
