# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `setting` column of `lichen_biomonitoring.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: nitrogen_pct, sulfur_pct, lead_mg_kg, zinc_mg_kg, chla_phaeo_ratio, lichen_cover_pct.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
