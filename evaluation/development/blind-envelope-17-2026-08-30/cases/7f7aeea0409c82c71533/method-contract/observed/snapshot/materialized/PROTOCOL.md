# Study protocol

This study compares the two groups recorded in the `pond_type` column of `damselfly_condition.csv`.
The pre-declared outcome family, in this fixed order, is: body_length_mm, hindwing_length_mm, abdominal_fat_mg, mite_count, encapsulation_grey.
Each outcome is compared between the two groups. The named outcomes form one outcome
family; per-outcome comparisons over this family require complete-family control of the
family-wise error from multiple comparisons.
