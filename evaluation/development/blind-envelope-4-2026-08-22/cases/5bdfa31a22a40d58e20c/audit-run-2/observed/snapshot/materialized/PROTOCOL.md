# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `PFRatio` between the two levels of `SedationArm` (`light` and `deep`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the patient. The column `PatientID` carries the value that
identifies it: each patient has one distinct `PatientID` value. The sedation protocol was applied to the individual patient, so the patient is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `PFRatio` between the two levels of `SedationArm`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
