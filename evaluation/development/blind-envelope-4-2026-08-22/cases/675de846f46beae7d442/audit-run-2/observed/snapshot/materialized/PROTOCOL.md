# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `body_mass_kg` between the two levels of `diet_group` (`standard` and `supplemented`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the seal pup. The column `pup_tag` carries the value that
identifies it: each seal pup has one distinct `pup_tag` value. The feeding protocol was assigned to the individual pup at admission, so the pup is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `body_mass_kg` between the two levels of `diet_group`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
