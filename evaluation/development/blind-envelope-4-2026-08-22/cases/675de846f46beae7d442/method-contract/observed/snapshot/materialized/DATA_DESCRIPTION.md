# Data description: harbour seal pup weekly weighings

## File

`seal_pup_masses.csv` — 160 data rows plus one header row, comma separated, UTF-8.

Generated by `make_data.py` (Python standard library only, fixed seed `SEED = 2`). Rerunning
`/usr/local/bin/python3 make_data.py` reproduces the same file byte for byte.

## What one row is

One row is one weekly weighing of one seal pup: the body mass recorded for a single pup in a
single week of care.

## Units and counts

- Units observed: 20 orphaned harbour seal pups, tagged `HS-101` through `HS-120`.
- Weighings per pup: 8, one in each of weeks 1 through 8 in care. No weighings are missing.
- Total rows: 20 pups x 8 weeks = 160 data rows.
- The 8 rows that share a `pup_tag` are successive weekly weighings of that same animal.

## Groups

Two supplementary feeding protocols, 10 pups (80 rows) each:

| `diet_group`  | Pups              | Rows | Description                                |
|---------------|-------------------|------|--------------------------------------------|
| `standard`    | HS-101 to HS-110  | 80   | The centre's standard pup diet              |
| `supplemented`| HS-111 to HS-120  | 80   | High-fat fish-oil supplemented diet          |

A pup keeps its assigned diet for all eight weeks, so `diet_group` is constant within a `pup_tag`.

## Columns, in file order

| # | Column          | Type    | Values / range in this file | Meaning |
|---|-----------------|---------|-----------------------------|---------|
| 1 | `pup_tag`       | string  | `HS-101` ... `HS-120` (20 distinct, 8 rows each) | Identifier of the individual pup weighed. |
| 2 | `diet_group`    | string  | `standard`, `supplemented` | Which feeding protocol that pup was assigned at admission. |
| 3 | `week_in_care`  | integer | 1 ... 8 | Which week of care the weighing was taken in; week 1 is the admission weighing. |
| 4 | `body_mass_kg`  | decimal | 16.0 to 32.1, one decimal place | Body mass of that pup at that weighing, in kilograms. |

## How the values were produced

For each pup an admission mass was drawn from a normal distribution with mean 18.4 kg and standard
deviation 2.0 kg. Mass then grows by a fixed weekly amount, 1.3 kg per week for standard-diet pups
and 1.7 kg per week for supplemented pups, and each individual weighing adds normal noise with
standard deviation 0.4 kg for scale error and gut fill. Every value is rounded to one decimal
place, floored at 13.0 kg, and forced to be at least as large as that pup's previous weighing, so
no pup appears to lose mass.

Resulting group means in this file: standard 22.79 kg across its 80 rows, supplemented 24.84 kg
across its 80 rows. Week 1 means are 18.4 kg (standard) and 19.0 kg (supplemented); week 8 means
are 27.3 kg (standard) and 30.8 kg (supplemented).
