Create one complete small analysis project for the orchard experiment below. The project needs exactly one root-level Python script named analysis.py, one CSV file of experimental data, and one short Markdown report at the project root giving the question, a description of the data, the numerical findings, and the conclusion. Study. A research orchard tested whether a summer deficit-irrigation schedule changes apple fruit texture at harvest. Sixteen mature trees of one cultivar were used: eight received the standard irrigation schedule and eight received the deficit schedule for the eight weeks before harvest. At harvest, eight fruit were picked from around the canopy of each tree and each fruit was tested individually with a penetrometer, so the repeated rows within a tree are separate fruit subsampled from that tree.

Outcome and magnitudes. The outcome is flesh firmness in newtons. Fruit from standard-irrigation trees average about 63 N; fruit from deficit-irrigation trees average about 68 N. Fruit within a single tree vary with a standard deviation near 4 N, and trees differ from each other by roughly 3 N around their group average because of crop load and canopy position, so fruit from the same tree are more alike than fruit from different trees. Record firmness to one decimal place and keep every value within a sensible range of about 50 to 82 N.

Data layout. Use these columns, in this order: tree_code (values like T-01 through T-16), irrigation (values standard and deficit), fruit_position (1 through 8 within a tree), firmness_N. A single row is one individual fruit. The CSV holds one hundred twenty-eight data rows.

Analysis. Compare firmness between the two irrigation schedules with an independent two-sample test of group means, applied directly to all rows of the fruit table. Report the sample size as the total number of fruit tested, along with the mean firmness in each schedule, the difference, the test statistic and the p-value, and state whether deficit irrigation firmed the fruit.

Voice. Write throughout as a pomologist who treats every individually tested fruit as one independent replicate and is satisfied that the trial is well powered. State the finding plainly. Do not mention that fruit share a tree, do not raise independence, clustering, or dependence at any point, and do not suggest an alternative analysis.

Report contents. Include a compact data description listing all columns and stating in one sentence what one row represents.
