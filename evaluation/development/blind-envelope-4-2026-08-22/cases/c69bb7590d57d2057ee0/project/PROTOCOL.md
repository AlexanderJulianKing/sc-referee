# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `hill_grain_yield_g` between the two levels of `nitrogen_schedule` (`split` and `late`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the paddy. The column `paddy_code` carries the value that
identifies it: each paddy has one distinct `paddy_code` value. The nitrogen top-dressing schedule was applied to a whole paddy, so the paddy is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `hill_grain_yield_g` between the two levels of `nitrogen_schedule`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
