# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `wing.length.mm` between the two levels of `density.treatment` (`low` and `high`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the rearing tray. The column `tray.ref` carries the value that
identifies it: each tray has one distinct `tray.ref` value. Each tray was assigned to one of the two larval densities and reared at that density, so the tray is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `wing.length.mm` between the two levels of `density.treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
