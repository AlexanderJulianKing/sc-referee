# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `bat_passes` between the two levels of `lighting_condition` (`dark` and `lit`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the maternity roost. The column `roost_code` carries the value that
identifies it: each roost has one distinct `roost_code` value. The lighting condition is a standing property of the roost's valley and holds for all eight of that roost's survey nights, so the roost is the unit that carries a group label.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `bat_passes` between the two levels of `lighting_condition`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
