# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `snout_vent_length_mm` between the two levels of `diet_treatment` (`standard_flake` and `flake_plus_daphnia`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the rearing bin. The column `bin_label` carries the value that
identifies it: each bin has one distinct `bin_label` value. Each rearing bin was assigned to one of the two diets, so the bin is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `snout_vent_length_mm` between the two levels of `diet_treatment`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
