# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `weaning_weight_g` between the two levels of `diet_group` (`standard` and `supplemented`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the breeding doe. The column `doe_id` carries the value that
identifies it: each doe has one distinct `doe_id` value. Each doe was fed one of the two rations from mating through lactation and raised one litter, so the doe is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `weaning_weight_g` between the two levels of `diet_group`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
