# Governing protocol

## Study question

Compare `head_fresh_mass_g` between the two levels of `formulation` (`standard` and `raised_potassium`).

## Independent experimental unit

The independent experimental unit is the nutrient-film gutter. The column `gutter_code` carries the value that
identifies it: each gutter has one distinct `gutter_code` value. Each gutter's reservoir was dosed with one of the two formulations for the whole crop, so the gutter is the unit that was assigned to a group.

## Analysis

The analysis is a two-group comparison of `head_fresh_mass_g` between the two levels of `formulation`.
This protocol does not select, require, or exclude any particular statistical procedure.
