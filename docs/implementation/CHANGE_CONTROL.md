# Change control

A change requires a new or superseding ADR when it alters any of these:

- what may be called a Finding;
- authority among code, report, scientist, and model assertions;
- eligibility of model-derived semantics;
- counterevidence obligations;
- execution or network privileges;
- canonical storage or semantic-lock meaning;
- public capability claims;
- detector maturity or promotion requirements.

A temporary implementation experiment may proceed without a full ADR only when it is:

1. clearly marked provisional;
2. outside the W3ID public schema namespace;
3. unable to weaken a safety invariant;
4. covered by a removal or promotion condition; and
5. recorded in the schema-gap or task register.
